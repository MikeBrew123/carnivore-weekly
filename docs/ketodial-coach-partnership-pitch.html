Reviewer Partnership for Keren

Here's the short version: we're building a text-based coaching product for people doing keto and carnivore. Members check in weekly, get personalized coaching responses, and stay accountable. AI writes the first draft. You make it actually good.

You're not a chatbot operator. You're the quality filter, the person who catches what the AI misses. Your carnivore experience is what makes this product real instead of just another app.

How It Works

The member experience: They pay $49/month. Every Sunday, they check in with a few quick numbers (weight, energy, cravings, adherence) and a sentence or two about their week. Within 24 hours, they get a personalized coaching response from "Coach Remy."

Behind the scenes: AI drafts the response using the member's full history, patterns, and goals. You review it, tweak it, approve it. Most drafts are solid. You're scanning for tone, accuracy, and anything the AI got wrong. Quick approvals take 30 to 60 seconds. Complex cases get your real attention.

Your identity stays private. Members see Coach Remy. You work behind the scenes, on your schedule, from home. Reviews batch after Sunday check-ins.

The Part That Gets Interesting

The AI learns from your edits. Every time you change a response, the system gets smarter. Month 1, you might edit 60% of drafts. By month 3, maybe 30%. By month 6, you're approving 80% with minor tweaks.

Your workload goes down while the member count goes up. That's the math that matters.

Boundaries and Quality Control

  • Medical questions are already handled. The AI routes anything medical to "talk to your doctor." You just confirm it happened correctly.
  • You can flag problem members. If someone is a time drain (constant messages, medical overreach, demanding daily attention on a weekly plan), we pause or offboard them. Your time is protected.
  • Written scope-of-practice protocol. The product doesn't give medical advice. That's explicit in the terms. You're coaching accountability, not treating conditions.

  • If this grows past 100 members and stays there, we incorporate properly and you come on as a partner. Real equity, real structure.
