## Why these four, and why the rest wait

The four changed in this pass all print the **2,600 mg / 3,400 mg universal
potassium target** - the identical figures already removed from CW drip day-10
(a5a77d43), KD drip day-2 (a0126df9) and the KetoDial keto flu guide (e576aa6).
Potassium alongside an ACE inhibitor, an ARB, spironolactone, a diuretic or CKD
is the combination that actually hurts someone, and the median known buyer is 65.

They are also, conveniently, the four lowest-risk pages to touch: 4 to 29
impressions each, and one with no GSC data at all. Total clicks at risk across
all four: **2**. So the safety fix and the ranking-preservation rule do not
conflict here.

What must not change on these four: URL, title, meta description, canonical, and
the salting-food-to-taste advice that is the reason people read them. Only the
prescribed milligram figures come out.

The remaining pages in the table carry magnesium or sodium dosing rather than
universal potassium targets. Lower risk, and several are genuine ranking assets
(adaptation-timeline at 1,358 impressions and position 6.5 is the most valuable
page in the set). They get their own pass with their own plan rather than being
swept into this one. Tracked on bead carnivore-weekly-drp9.

- **Alcohol:** impressions had already halved in the week of 09-08, before the
  edit went in on 09-14. The impressions lost were deep ones: before, the page's
  top queries included "alcohol on carnivore diet" at position 27. Average
  position improved to 7.0 and clicks did not fall. The page lost page-3
  exposure, not its page-one ranking. Not attributable to the sweep.
- **Best-macro-calculator, untouched**, fell 47% on impressions in the same
  window with a similar curve. Whatever moved the alcohol page also moved a page
  nobody edited. That is the control doing its job.
- **Endurance:** peaked at 27 impressions the week before the edit, then fell to
  1 in the week the edit landed, which was mostly pre-edit days. Zero clicks
  ever. Not attributable to the sweep, and nothing to protect.

### Overall verdict

**The sweep cost nothing measurable.** The protected asset, `adaptation-timeline`,
held (impressions and position both up, clicks flat). `fasting-protocols`, the
page that took both a tier-2 and a tier-3 change, improved the most. The two
impression drops began before the edit and are matched by an untouched control.
Tiers 1 to 3 cannot be separated from each other (same day, by design), and that
no longer matters because none of them shows harm.
