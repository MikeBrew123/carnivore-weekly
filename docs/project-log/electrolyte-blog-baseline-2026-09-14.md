## Why these four, and why the rest wait

The four changed in this pass all print the **2,600 mg / 3,400 mg universal
potassium target** - the identical figures already removed from CW drip day-10
(a5a77d43), KD drip day-2 (a0126df9) and the KetoDial keto flu guide (e576aa6).
Potassium alongside an ACE inhibitor, an ARB, spironolactone, a diuretic or CKD
is the combination that actually hurts someone, and the median known buyer is 65.

They are also, conveniently, the four lowest-risk pages to touch: 4 to 29
impressions each, and one with no GSC data at all. Total clicks at risk across
all four: **2**. So the safety fix and the ranking-preservation rule do not
conflict here.

What must not change on these four: URL, title, meta description, canonical, and
the salting-food-to-taste advice that is the reason people read them. Only the
prescribed milligram figures come out.

The remaining pages in the table carry magnesium or sodium dosing rather than
universal potassium targets. Lower risk, and several are genuine ranking assets
(adaptation-timeline at 1,358 impressions and position 6.5 is the most valuable
page in the set). They get their own pass with their own plan rather than being
swept into this one. Tracked on bead carnivore-weekly-drp9.
