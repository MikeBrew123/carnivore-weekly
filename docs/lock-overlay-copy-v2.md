## Notes from Sarah on what changed and why

The big shift is reframing this screen from "pay or leave" to "you're partway through." The 12 remaining personalization questions are now the headline feature, not hidden behind a toggle, which kills the bait-and-switch and makes $29 feel like an investment in specificity. Every bullet starts with YOUR and references something the reader actually told us (bodyweight, goals, symptoms), so it reads like a tailored plan instead of a PDF download. I rewrote the guarantee so it sounds like a real promise from a real person, not legal fine print, and I itemized the $298 so the anchor finally lands on the visible overlay instead of getting buried in the React bundle.
