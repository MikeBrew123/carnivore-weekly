Priority: HIGH

12.5% conversion is excellent, but you can improve the 10% payment modal → paid report conversion:

Priority: MEDIUM

With 70.5% bounce rate, users are engaged. Now optimize for search:

Priority: HIGH

You have 116 users but likely <100 email subscribers. Capture them:
