    This calculator is for adults

    PescoDial's calculator is not available for anyone under 18. Growing bodies need different amounts of food, and a general calculator can't account for that.

    If you're under 18 and thinking about changing how you eat, a doctor or school nurse is a good person to talk to first.

    We can't give a weight-loss target

    Based on your height and weight, you are already below the usual healthy weight range. Losing more weight could be harmful, so we won't suggest a calorie target for weight loss.

    We can show you numbers for keeping your weight steady. If you've lost weight without trying, please mention it to your doctor.

    We can't give a self-guided target

    Your estimated daily needs are at or below the lowest amount we use for a self-guided plan, so a calculator is the wrong tool here. Your doctor or a registered dietitian can set numbers that fit you.

      How this calculator works

      This calculator estimates how many calories your body uses in a day with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. It starts with your age, sex, height and weight, then multiplies by your activity level: 1.2 if you're sedentary, 1.375 for light activity, 1.55 for moderate activity and 1.725 if you're very active.

      If you choose weight loss, the calculator sets your daily target 20% below your estimated needs. It never goes below 1,200 calories a day for women or 1,500 for men. If your needs are already at or below that floor, or you're already under a healthy weight, it won't give you a weight-loss target. Instead, it suggests talking with your doctor or a registered dietitian.

      Protein is set from your goal weight. Pescatarian Mediterranean aims for about 1.2 grams of protein per kilogram of goal weight. Pescatarian Low Carb, Pescatarian Keto and Pescatarian Carnivore aim for about 2 grams per kilogram of goal weight.

      Carbs depend on the eating style you pick. Pescatarian Mediterranean sets fat at about 35% of your calories, and carbs make up the rest. Pescatarian Low Carb allows up to about 100 grams of carbs a day, Pescatarian Keto about 20 grams, and Pescatarian Carnivore close to zero.

      These numbers are estimates for generally healthy adults, not medical advice. If you take any medicine, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a heart, kidney or other health condition, please talk to your doctor before you change how you eat.

      Source: Mifflin MD, St Jeor ST, et al. A new predictive equation for resting energy expenditure in healthy individuals. American Journal of Clinical Nutrition. 1990;51(2):241-247.
