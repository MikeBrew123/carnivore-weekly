Low-mercury guide

Based on the FDA and EPA advice about eating fish. Use it when you shop or order out.

Best choices

Eat often
  • Salmon
  • Sardines
  • Shrimp
  • Cod, haddock, pollock
  • Trout
  • Tilapia
  • Anchovies, herring
  • Scallops, clams, oysters
  • Canned light tuna
  • Atlantic mackerel

Good choices

1 serving a week
  • Albacore (white) tuna
  • Yellowfin tuna
  • Halibut
  • Mahi mahi
  • Snapper
  • Grouper
  • Chilean sea bass

Avoid

Highest in mercury
  • King mackerel
  • Marlin
  • Orange roughy
  • Shark
  • Swordfish
  • Tilefish (Gulf of Mexico)
  • Bigeye tuna

If you eat fish caught by family or friends, check local advisories for that lake, river or coast.

Section five

Five easy recipes

Each serves one. Double everything to cook for two or to save a portion for tomorrow.

Photo: salmon
Recipe 1 · Monday dinner

Sheet-pan salmon with lemon and green beans

25 minServes 11 sheet pan

You need

  • 5 oz salmon fillet
  • 1½ cups green beans
  • ½ cup small potatoes, halved
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ lemon, sliced
  • Salt, pepper, garlic

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes in half the oil and roast 10 minutes.
  2. Add green beans and salmon. Drizzle with the rest of the oil and season.
  3. Top salmon with lemon. Roast 12 to 14 minutes, until it flakes easily.

Swap: Trout or arctic char cook in the same time.

Photo: sardine toast
Recipe 2 · Tuesday lunch

Sardine and white bean toast

10 minServes 1No cooking

You need

  • 1 can sardines in olive oil
  • ½ cup white beans, rinsed
  • 1 slice whole-grain bread
  • ½ lemon
  • Parsley, red onion, pepper

Steps

  1. Toast the bread. Mash the beans with lemon and a pinch of salt.
  2. Spread beans on the toast. Lay sardines on top.
  3. Finish with parsley, thin onion slices and black pepper.

Swap: Canned light tuna or salmon instead of sardines.

Photo: shrimp skillet
Recipe 3 · Wednesday dinner

Shrimp and chickpea skillet

20 minServes 11 skillet

You need

  • 5 oz peeled shrimp
  • ½ cup chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1 cup sliced bell pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 clove garlic
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika

Steps

  1. Warm oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook peppers 5 minutes.
  2. Add garlic, paprika and chickpeas. Stir 2 minutes.
  3. Add shrimp and cook 2 to 3 minutes per side, until pink. Finish with lemon.

Swap: Scallops work the same way.

Photo: baked cod
Recipe 4 · Thursday dinner

Baked cod with tomatoes and olives

25 minServes 11 baking dish

You need

  • 6 oz cod fillet
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 6 Kalamata olives
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ½ cup cooked farro

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Toss tomatoes, olives, oil and oregano in a dish.
  2. Nestle the cod in the middle and season.
  3. Bake 15 to 18 minutes. Spoon the juices over the fish and serve with farro.

Swap: Haddock, pollock or tilapia.

Photo: yogurt bowl
Recipe 5 · Breakfast, any day

Greek yogurt breakfast bowl

5 minServes 1No cooking

You need

  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup berries
  • 2 tbsp walnuts
  • 1 tsp chia seeds
  • Pinch of cinnamon

Steps

  1. Spoon yogurt into a bowl.
  2. Top with berries, walnuts, chia and cinnamon.
  3. Frozen berries are fine. Let them sit 5 minutes to soften.

Swap: Unsweetened soy yogurt if you avoid dairy.

Eating out and travel

Most restaurants already serve something that fits your plan. A few simple requests make it easier.

At a restaurant

  • Look for grilled, baked, broiled or steamed fish.
  • Ask for vegetables or salad in place of fries.
  • Order sauces and dressings on the side.
  • Check the mercury guide before ordering swordfish or tuna steak.
  • Box half your meal when portions are large.

Easy menu picks

  • Italian: grilled fish, minestrone, salad.
  • Greek: grilled fish or shrimp, Greek salad, hummus.
  • Japanese: salmon or cod dishes, miso soup, edamame.
  • Mexican: fish or shrimp tacos, black beans, salsa.
  • Diner: eggs, fruit, whole-grain toast.

When you travel

  • Pack nuts, fruit and tuna or salmon pouches.
  • At hotel breakfasts, choose eggs, yogurt and fruit.
  • Grocery stores sell ready salads and cooked shrimp.
  • Carry a refillable water bottle.
  • Get back to your plan at the next meal.

What to say when you order

Could I have the fish grilled, with vegetables instead of the fries?
Is the fish cooked in butter or olive oil? Olive oil, please, if you can.
