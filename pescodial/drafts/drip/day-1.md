Let's start with the numbers you just saw.

**Protein** is listed in grams per day. It's worked out from your goal weight, not your current weight, so it stays sensible if you have weight to lose. On Pescatarian Mediterranean, the lead style, that's about 1.2 grams for every kilogram of goal weight. Fish, shrimp, eggs, beans and lentils all count toward it.

**Fat** is shown as grams and as a share of your calories. On Pescatarian Mediterranean it sits around 35%, and most of it is meant to come from olive oil, with the rest from fish, nuts and seeds.

**Carbs** fill whatever is left: vegetables, fruit, legumes and whole grains.

If you picked a different style, the shape changes:

- **Pescatarian Keto** keeps carbs very low and lets fat carry most of your energy.
- **Pescatarian Low Carb** sits in the middle, with more room for vegetables and some fruit.
- **Pescatarian Carnivore** is fish, seafood and eggs, with almost no plant foods.

Here's how I'd use them. Treat your numbers as a starting sketch, not a test you pass or fail. Most people do well just aiming to land somewhere near protein most days and letting the rest settle. You don't need to weigh every meal this week. Notice what a normal day looks like first.

**Before you change anything, talk to your doctor.** This matters most if you take a blood thinner, blood pressure medicine or diabetes medicine. Changing what you eat can change how those medicines work, and your doctor may want to adjust a dose or check your bloodwork sooner. Bring your calculator numbers to the appointment. They make a good conversation starter.

You can run the calculator again any time, and it's worth doing if your weight or goal changes: https://www.pescodial.com/calculator/
