Subject: Your one-month check-in
Preheader: Four quick questions, a fresh look at your numbers, and what comes next.

Hi,

It's been about a month since you first ran the PescoDial calculator. Before we look ahead, take two minutes to look back. No scoring, no guilt. Just notice.

**Four questions to ask yourself:**

1. **How many fish meals did I eat in a typical week?** Zero to two is a fine start. Three or four means the habit is forming.
2. **Am I landing near my protein number most days?** If not, which meal is the gap? It's usually breakfast.
3. **What was easy?** Do more of that. If canned salmon lunches stuck, that's your anchor.
4. **What kept getting in the way?** Cost, time, a family that wasn't keen, not liking the taste of fish? Name it, because that's the thing to solve next.

**Re-run your numbers.** If your weight, goal or activity has changed, your targets may have too. It takes a minute: https://www.pescodial.com/calculator/

**Thinking about switching styles?** That's allowed. Some people start with Pescatarian Mediterranean and later try Pescatarian Low Carb, or the other way round. Pescatarian Keto and Pescatarian Carnivore are bigger changes. Whatever you choose, run the calculator again so your numbers match the style.

**Check in with your doctor.** If you've changed how you eat this month, it's a good moment to book a follow-up, especially if you take a blood thinner, blood pressure medicine or diabetes medicine. Ask whether any bloodwork or a medication review makes sense now. Please don't change a dose yourself.

**What comes next.** You'll keep hearing from me now and then with practical notes. Our guides are growing, and you can browse them any time: https://www.pescodial.com/guides/

Recipes, a printable list, budget help, cooking for one, understanding your cholesterol numbers well enough to talk them over with your doctor: whatever it is, I read every reply, and it shapes what we build.

Thank you for spending this month with me. Eating more fish doesn't need to be complicated, and you've already done the hardest part, which is starting.
