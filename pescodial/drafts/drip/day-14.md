Subject: Protein after 50, and why it matters
Preheader: Keeping your muscle is one of the kindest things you can do for future you.

Hi,

Two weeks in. Today's topic gets more important every birthday: protein, and the muscle it helps you keep.

**Why muscle matters.** We tend to lose muscle and strength as we age. That's normal, but it isn't only about how you look. Muscle helps you carry groceries, climb stairs, get up from a low chair and catch yourself if you trip. Keeping it is about staying independent.

**Protein is part of the answer.** An international expert group (PROT-AGE) reviewed the research and concluded that older adults likely need more protein than younger adults to hold on to muscle, not less. That's why PescoDial works out your protein from your goal weight, and why I keep pointing you back to that number. It's your target, not a universal one.

**Spread it out.** A lot of us eat a light breakfast, a small lunch and most of our protein at dinner. Try moving some of it earlier. Ideas:

- **Breakfast:** eggs, smoked salmon, plain Greek yogurt, or leftover fish from last night
- **Lunch:** a can of salmon, sardines or tuna on salad, or a bean and shrimp bowl
- **Dinner:** your fish portion, sized to your number

If you're well under your protein number most days, add one thing at breakfast before you try to fix everything else.

**Muscle needs a reason to stay.** Protein works best alongside using your muscles. The US Physical Activity Guidelines include muscle-strengthening activity on two or more days a week for adults, plus balance work for older adults. That can mean resistance bands, light weights, sit-to-stands from a kitchen chair, or carrying the shopping in. Start where you are, and ask your doctor what's safe for you.

**By style:**

- **Pescatarian Mediterranean:** fish and eggs do most of the work, with beans and lentils adding more.
- **Pescatarian Keto and Low Carb:** protein usually comes easily from fish and eggs. Just make sure fat isn't crowding it off the plate.
- **Pescatarian Carnivore:** hitting protein is rarely the problem. Spreading it over the day still helps.

**Important:** if you have kidney disease or have been told to limit protein, don't use the calculator's protein number. Ask your doctor or a registered dietitian what's right for you. And please talk to your doctor before changing your diet if you take a blood thinner, blood pressure medicine or diabetes medicine.
