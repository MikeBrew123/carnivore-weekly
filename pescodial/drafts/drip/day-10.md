Subject: The budget hero in your pantry
Preheader: Sardines, salmon and mackerel in a can, plus a 3-day easy menu.

Hi,

Fresh fish is lovely. It's also pricey, and it doesn't wait for you if your week goes sideways. That's where the can comes in.

**Why canned fish earns its shelf space:**

- **It keeps.** Months in the cupboard, ready when you are.
- **It's already cooked.** Open, drain, eat.
- **Sardines, salmon and mackerel are oily fish,** the kind rich in omega-3 fats.
- **Bones count.** Canned sardines and canned salmon with the soft bones left in are a source of calcium. Mash the bones in with a fork and you won't notice them.
- **It's mostly low in mercury.** Sardines, salmon and canned light tuna are on the FDA/EPA best choices list. With mackerel, check the label for the species, since king mackerel is one to avoid.

**Two label checks:** sodium varies a lot from brand to brand, so if you watch your blood pressure, compare labels or look for "no salt added." And fish packed in olive oil adds to your fat number, while fish in water doesn't.

**A 3-day easy menu (Pescatarian Mediterranean):**

**Day 1**
- Breakfast: two eggs, tomato and a slice of whole grain toast
- Lunch: canned salmon mashed with lemon, olive oil and chopped celery, on greens
- Dinner: whole wheat pasta with sardines, garlic, olive oil, chili flakes and spinach

**Day 2**
- Breakfast: plain yogurt with walnuts and berries
- Lunch: canned light tuna and white bean salad with red onion and parsley
- Dinner: salmon patties (canned salmon, an egg, a few breadcrumbs) with a big salad

**Day 3**
- Breakfast: leftover salmon patty with a fried egg
- Lunch: mackerel on rye crackers with cucumber and a squeeze of lemon
- Dinner: lentil and vegetable soup, with sardines on toast on the side

Adjust the portions to your calculator protein number. Most of these scale up or down with one more or one fewer can.

**Other styles:**

- **Pescatarian Keto or Low Carb:** skip the pasta, toast and crackers. Serve the fish over greens or with avocado, and swap the lentil soup for a vegetable soup.
- **Pescatarian Carnivore:** canned fish and eggs are your easiest meals. Rotate sardines, salmon, mackerel and tuna through the week.

Please talk to your doctor before changing your diet, especially if you take a blood thinner, blood pressure medicine or diabetes medicine. If sodium is a concern for you, your doctor can tell you how closely to watch it.
