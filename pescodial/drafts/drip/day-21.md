Subject: Eating out and travelling pescatarian
Preheader: How to order, what to pack, and the one menu fish to skip.

Hi,

Three weeks in. Home cooking is the easy part. Restaurants, road trips and visiting family are where plans wobble, so here's how to keep yours steady without being "difficult."

**At a restaurant:**

- **Look for the cooking word.** Grilled, baked, broiled, steamed and poached are your friends. Battered, crispy and fried mean a different meal.
- **Ask for the swap.** Most kitchens will trade fries for vegetables or a side salad if you ask. It's a normal request.
- **Sauce on the side.** Then you decide how much.
- **Olive oil and lemon.** Ask for them with grilled fish. It's the Mediterranean plate in two words.
- **Skip swordfish and shark,** which turn up on menus and are on the FDA/EPA avoid list. With tuna steaks or sushi, it's fair to ask which tuna it is, since bigeye is on the avoid list too.

**By style at the table:**

- **Pescatarian Mediterranean:** almost any seafood restaurant, Greek, Italian or Spanish place will have something that fits.
- **Pescatarian Keto or Low Carb:** order the fish, double the vegetables, pass on the bread basket and the rice.
- **Pescatarian Carnivore:** grilled fish, shrimp or eggs, with a plain side of more of the same. Ask that it's cooked without breading.

**On the road:**

- Pack a few tins of sardines, salmon or tuna with pull-tab lids, and a fork.
- Hard-boiled eggs travel well for a day in a cooler bag.
- Hotel breakfasts nearly always have eggs, and often smoked salmon.
- Grocery stores beat airport food courts. Look for pre-cooked shrimp, canned fish and bagged salad.

**Visiting family:** offer to bring a dish. A tray of baked salmon or a big shrimp salad means there's always one thing on the table that works for you, and nobody has to cook around you.

**A note on fish oil.** If you're tempted to cover travel weeks with omega-3 supplements, check with your doctor first. Fish oil might affect how your blood clots when it's taken with a blood thinner such as warfarin. The research is mixed, which is exactly why it's worth asking first. Don't add a supplement without asking, especially if you take a blood thinner, blood pressure medicine or diabetes medicine.

One missed meal doesn't undo anything. Get back to your usual plate at the next one.
