## Verified, no change needed

- **Day 5 species lists:** every listed species matches its FDA/EPA list (best, good, avoid). That includes Pacific chub mackerel, skipjack, whiting, sole, Chilean sea bass, Spanish mackerel and Gulf of Mexico tilefish. Pregnancy and breastfeeding: 8-12 oz a week, 2-3 servings from best choices or 1 from good choices. Adult serving is 4 oz measured before cooking. A good-choice serving means no other fish that week. Children's portions are listed by age. Locally caught fish with no advisory: one serving and no other fish that week. https://www.fda.gov/food/consumers/advice-about-eating-fish
- **Day 10:** canned sardines and salmon with bones as calcium sources (https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Calcium-HealthProfessional/). Salmon, sardines and mackerel are oily, omega-3-rich fish (ODS omega-3 sheet). Sardines, salmon and canned light tuna are best choices, and king mackerel is on the avoid list (FDA).
- **Day 21:** swordfish, shark and bigeye tuna are on the avoid list (FDA).
- **Day 3:** AHA says eat fish twice a week; a serving is 3.5 oz cooked or about 3/4 cup flaked (heart.org news, 2018). The oily and white fish examples are all best choices.
- **Day 4:** AHA egg advice: up to one whole egg a day for healthy people, up to two for older adults with normal cholesterol. People respond to dietary cholesterol differently. Shrimp is a best choice. Shrimp is high in cholesterol but very low in saturated fat (general nutrition data, consistent with the advisory).
- **Day 7:** PREDIMED: Spain, 7,447 people aged 55-80 at high cardiovascular risk. The control group got advice to reduce dietary fat. The endpoint was heart attack, stroke or cardiovascular death. Both Mediterranean arms had fewer events. Olive oil is about 13.5 g of fat per tablespoon (USDA FoodData Central), so "roughly 14" holds.
- **Day 14:** PROT-AGE: Bauer J et al., JAMDA 2013;14(8):542-559. Older adults need more protein than younger adults, and the kidney-disease exception is consistent with the email's warning. PAG 2nd ed. 2018: muscle strengthening on 2 or more days a week, and older adults should do multicomponent activity that includes balance training. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23867520/ ; https://health.gov/sites/default/files/2019-10/PAG_ExecutiveSummary.pdf
- **Day 1:** protein 1.2 g/kg of goal weight and fat around 35% match the approved calculator spec (docs/project-log/decisions.md, 2026-10-03).
