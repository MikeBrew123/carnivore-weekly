# Contact

Have a question about the calculator or your plan? Email us at [pescodial@carnivoreweekly.com](mailto:pescodial@carnivoreweekly.com). A real person reads every message.

## Refunds

If the 7-day plan isn't right for you, you can ask for a refund within 30 days. Just reply to your receipt email, or write to the address above.

## One thing we can't do

We can't give medical advice by email. If your question is about a health condition, a medicine or symptoms, please take it to your doctor. They know your full picture, and we don't.
