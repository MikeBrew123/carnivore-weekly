# Questions for your doctor

<!-- Section intro. Each question gets a ruled answer line in the layout. -->

Bring this page to your next appointment. Write the answers on the lines.

1. Is this eating style a good fit for my health and the medicines I take?
2. What daily calories and protein should I aim for?
3. Which blood tests should I have before I start, and when should we check them again? (For example: cholesterol, blood sugar, kidney function.)
4. My kidneys: is it safe for me to eat more protein than I do now?
5. Do any of my medicines need to be watched or adjusted if I change how I eat, especially my blood pressure or diabetes medicines?
6. If I eat fewer carbs, could any of my diabetes medicines make my blood sugar drop too low?
7. I take a blood thinner. Should I keep leafy greens, olive oil and other foods steady from week to week?
8. Is a fish oil or omega-3 supplement safe with my medicines, especially a blood thinner?
9. Do any of my medicines interact with grapefruit or grapefruit juice?
10. Do I need vitamin D, B12, calcium or another supplement on this eating style?

If you're pregnant, breastfeeding or might become pregnant, add: Which fish should I eat, how much, and what should I avoid?
