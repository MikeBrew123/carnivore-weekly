## Pan-seared salmon with garlic butter spinach

**Meal:** Dinner · **Time:** 20 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 skillet

**You need**
- 4 oz salmon fillet, skin on
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 clove garlic, sliced
- 3 cups fresh spinach
- ½ lemon, salt and pepper

**Steps**
1. Pat the salmon dry and season with salt and pepper.
2. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the salmon skin side down for 4 to 5 minutes, until the skin is crisp.
3. Flip and cook 2 to 4 minutes more, until it flakes easily. Move it to a plate.
4. Lower the heat, add the butter and garlic, then the spinach. Stir until the spinach wilts, about 2 minutes.
5. Serve the salmon on the spinach with a squeeze of lemon.

**Swap:** Trout or Atlantic mackerel fillets.

## Tuna-stuffed avocado

**Meal:** Lunch · **Time:** 10 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 bowl, no cooking

**You need**
- 1 can light tuna (about 5 oz), drained
- 1 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 celery stalk, finely chopped
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 ripe avocado
- Fresh dill or chives, salt and pepper

**Steps**
1. Mix the tuna, mayonnaise, celery and lemon juice in a bowl. Season with salt and pepper.
2. Halve the avocado and remove the pit.
3. Pile the tuna into the avocado halves.
4. Top with dill or chives.

**Swap:** Canned salmon instead of tuna. Choose light tuna over albacore to keep mercury lower.

## Garlic shrimp with zucchini noodles

**Meal:** Dinner · **Time:** 20 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 skillet

**You need**
- 4 oz peeled shrimp, thawed if frozen
- 2 cups zucchini noodles (store-bought or spiralized)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp butter
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- Pinch of red pepper flakes
- 1 tbsp grated parmesan, salt and pepper

**Steps**
1. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and pepper flakes and cook 1 minute.
2. Add the shrimp and cook 2 to 3 minutes per side, until pink and opaque. Move them to a plate.
3. Add the butter and zucchini noodles to the pan. Toss for 2 to 3 minutes, just until warm.
4. Return the shrimp, season, and top with parmesan.

**Swap:** Scallops instead of shrimp.

## Parmesan-crusted cod with roasted broccoli

**Meal:** Dinner · **Time:** 25 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 sheet pan

**You need**
- 4 oz cod fillet
- 1½ cups broccoli florets
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp grated parmesan
- 1 tbsp butter, melted
- ½ tsp dried parsley or oregano, salt and pepper

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the broccoli with the oil and a pinch of salt, spread it on the pan and roast for 8 minutes.
2. Mix the parmesan, melted butter and herbs.
3. Put the cod on the pan, season it, and press the parmesan mixture on top.
4. Roast 12 to 15 minutes, until the cod flakes easily and the topping is golden.

**Swap:** Haddock or pollock.

## Smoked salmon scrambled eggs

**Meal:** Breakfast · **Time:** 10 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 nonstick skillet

**You need**
- 3 eggs
- 1 oz smoked salmon, torn into pieces
- 1 tbsp cream cheese
- 1 tsp butter
- Fresh chives and black pepper

**Steps**
1. Beat the eggs with a pinch of pepper. Smoked salmon is salty, so go easy on salt.
2. Melt the butter in the skillet over low heat. Add the eggs.
3. Stir slowly until the eggs are softly set and cooked through, 3 to 4 minutes.
4. Take the pan off the heat. Fold in the cream cheese and salmon.
5. Top with chives.

**Swap:** Leftover cooked salmon or trout in place of smoked salmon.
