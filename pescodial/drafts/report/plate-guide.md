# Build every plate the same way

<!-- The plate guide that sits under the numbers (or under the hidden-numbers note). Pick the block for the reader's style. A palm-sized piece of fish is about 4 ounces before cooking, per the FDA and EPA. -->

## Pescatarian Mediterranean

- Half the plate: vegetables or salad
- A quarter: fish, eggs or other protein, about the size of your palm
- A quarter: whole grains, beans or potatoes
- Finish with a drizzle of olive oil and a glass of water.

## Pescatarian Keto

- Most of the plate: fish, seafood or eggs, about the size of your palm or a little more
- The rest: leafy greens or other low-carb vegetables
- Add fat you can see: olive oil, butter, avocado or a little cheese.
- Skip the bread, rice, pasta, beans and potatoes.

## Pescatarian Low Carb

- Half the plate: non-starchy vegetables or salad
- A quarter to a half: fish, seafood or eggs, about the size of your palm
- A small handful of nuts, seeds or cheese if you're still hungry
- Leave out bread, rice, pasta, beans and potatoes.

## Pescatarian Carnivore

- Fish, seafood or eggs make up the plate.
- Start with a palm-sized portion and add more if you're still hungry.
- Cook in butter or in the fish's own fat, and salt to taste.
- A little cheese or plain yogurt is fine if dairy suits you.
