## Sardine Greek salad

**Meal:** Lunch · **Time:** 10 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 bowl, no cooking

**You need**
- 1 can sardines in olive oil (about 3.75 oz), drained
- 2 cups chopped romaine
- 1 cup chopped cucumber
- ½ cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 6 Kalamata olives
- 1 oz feta, crumbled
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, ½ lemon, dried oregano

**Steps**
1. Put the romaine, cucumber, tomatoes and olives in a bowl.
2. Whisk the oil, lemon juice and a pinch of oregano.
3. Toss the salad with the dressing.
4. Top with the sardines and feta.

**Swap:** Canned salmon or two hard-boiled eggs.

## Shrimp stir-fry with cauliflower rice

**Meal:** Dinner · **Time:** 20 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 large skillet

**You need**
- 4 oz peeled shrimp, thawed if frozen
- 1 cup riced cauliflower (fresh or frozen)
- 1 cup broccoli florets
- ½ cup sliced bell pepper
- 1 tbsp olive or avocado oil
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger and 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce or tamari

**Steps**
1. Heat half the oil in the skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the cauliflower rice 4 to 5 minutes, then move it to a plate.
2. Add the rest of the oil, the broccoli and the pepper. Stir-fry 3 to 4 minutes.
3. Add the ginger, garlic and shrimp. Cook 3 to 4 minutes, until the shrimp are pink and opaque.
4. Stir in the soy sauce and serve over the cauliflower rice.

**Swap:** Scallops or cubes of firm white fish.

## Lemon-herb trout with roasted cauliflower

**Meal:** Dinner · **Time:** 30 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 sheet pan

**You need**
- 4 oz trout fillet
- 1½ cups cauliflower florets
- 1 cup green beans, trimmed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ lemon, sliced
- Fresh dill or parsley, salt and pepper

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the cauliflower and green beans with most of the oil and some salt. Roast 15 minutes.
2. Move the vegetables to the edges. Add the trout, brush with the rest of the oil and season.
3. Lay the lemon slices on the fish. Roast 8 to 10 minutes, until it flakes easily.
4. Scatter the herbs over everything.

**Swap:** Salmon, which may need a few minutes more.

## Spinach, pepper and feta frittata

**Meal:** Breakfast · **Time:** 15 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 small oven-safe skillet

**You need**
- 3 eggs
- 1 cup fresh spinach
- ¼ cup diced bell pepper
- 1 oz feta, crumbled
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper

**Steps**
1. Turn on the broiler. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper.
2. Heat the oil in the skillet over medium heat. Cook the pepper 2 minutes, then add the spinach until it wilts.
3. Pour in the eggs and scatter the feta on top. Cook without stirring for 3 to 4 minutes, until the edges set.
4. Slide the pan under the broiler for 1 to 2 minutes, until the top is set and cooked through.

**Swap:** Leftover cooked salmon instead of feta.

## Salmon lettuce wraps with crunchy slaw

**Meal:** Lunch or dinner · **Time:** 15 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 bowl

**You need**
- 4 oz cooked salmon (leftover or canned), flaked
- 1 cup shredded cabbage or coleslaw mix
- 2 tbsp plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp lime or lemon juice
- 4 large lettuce leaves (butter lettuce or romaine)
- ¼ avocado, sliced
- Salsa, salt and pepper

**Steps**
1. Mix the yogurt, lime juice and a pinch of salt. Toss it with the cabbage.
2. Lay out the lettuce leaves.
3. Fill each one with salmon, slaw and avocado.
4. Top with a spoonful of salsa and fold to eat.

**Swap:** Cooked shrimp or canned light tuna.
