# Five easy recipes: Pescatarian Carnivore

Each recipe serves one. Double everything to cook for two or to save a portion for tomorrow. If your meal plan lists a different portion, use the one in your plan. Salt to taste, and add a pinch of your favorite spice if you like.

## Butter-basted salmon with crispy skin

**Meal:** Dinner · **Time:** 15 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 skillet

**You need**
- 4 to 6 oz salmon fillet, skin on
- 1 tbsp butter
- Salt and black pepper

**Steps**
1. Pat the salmon dry, especially the skin. Season both sides with salt.
2. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Lay the salmon in skin side down with no oil and press it flat for 10 seconds.
3. Cook 4 to 5 minutes, until the skin is crisp and releases easily.
4. Flip, add the butter, and tilt the pan to spoon the melted butter over the fish for 2 to 3 minutes, until it flakes easily.

**Swap:** Trout or arctic char.

## Sardines with soft-scrambled eggs

**Meal:** Breakfast or lunch · **Time:** 10 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 nonstick skillet

**You need**
- 3 eggs
- 1 can sardines in water or their own oil (about 3.75 oz), drained
- 1 tsp butter
- Salt

**Steps**
1. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt.
2. Melt the butter in the skillet over low heat and add the eggs.
3. Stir slowly until the eggs are softly set and cooked through, 3 to 4 minutes.
4. Slide the eggs onto a plate. Warm the sardines in the same pan for 1 minute and lay them on top.

**Swap:** Canned salmon or smoked trout.

## Brown butter shrimp

**Meal:** Dinner · **Time:** 10 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 skillet

**You need**
- 5 oz peeled shrimp, thawed if frozen
- 1½ tbsp butter
- ½ tsp smoked paprika (optional)
- Salt

**Steps**
1. Pat the shrimp dry and season with salt and paprika.
2. Melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat. Let it bubble until it smells nutty and turns light brown, about 2 minutes.
3. Add the shrimp in a single layer. Cook 2 to 3 minutes per side, until pink and opaque.
4. Pour the pan butter over the shrimp to serve.

**Swap:** Sea scallops, seared 2 minutes per side.

## Salmon patties

**Meal:** Lunch or dinner · **Time:** 20 minutes · **Serves:** 1 (makes 2 patties) · **You'll use:** 1 bowl, 1 skillet

**You need**
- 1 can salmon (about 5 oz), drained
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp finely grated parmesan
- 1 tbsp butter
- Salt and pepper

**Steps**
1. Flake the salmon into a bowl. Canned salmon bones are soft, so you can mash them in or lift them out.
2. Add the egg, parmesan and a pinch of salt and pepper. Mix well.
3. Shape into 2 patties, about ¾ inch thick.
4. Melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the patties 3 to 4 minutes per side, until golden and hot all the way through.

**Swap:** Canned light tuna or cooked crab instead of salmon.

## Baked cod with parmesan butter

**Meal:** Dinner · **Time:** 20 minutes · **Serves:** 1 · **You'll use:** 1 small baking dish

**You need**
- 5 oz cod fillet
- 1 tbsp butter, softened
- 2 tbsp grated parmesan
- Salt and pepper

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Mash the butter and parmesan together.
3. Put the cod in the baking dish, season it, and spread the parmesan butter over the top.
4. Bake 12 to 15 minutes, until the cod flakes easily and the top is golden.

**Swap:** Haddock or pollock. If you skip cheese, bake the fish with just butter and salt.
