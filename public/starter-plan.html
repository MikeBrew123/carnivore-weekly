I'm not a doctor and this plan isn't medical advice. Dropping carbs this fast changes how insulin, sulfonylureas, and blood pressure medication behave, sometimes within the first few days, so if you take any of those, talk to whoever prescribes them before you start. Same goes if you're pregnant, nursing, or have kidney trouble, because the "eat more salt" advice in these emails isn't written for you. As always, check with your doctor before starting something new.
