This Week's Roundup

- Chloe, Community Scout

Week of July 19, 2026

Okay so this week the community went absolutely feral for bread replacements, and honestly I'm here for it. Steak and Butter Gal dropped a video on what she eats instead of bread and it pulled almost 2 million views, the comments section turned into a full-on recipe swap meet. People were sharing chicken tortilla hacks, butter powder crepes, you name it. There's something kind of beautiful about watching carnivores get genuinely excited about wrapping meat in. Different meat? The top comment demanding a thin and crispy pizza crust recipe had 71 likes, so clearly we've got unfinished business there.

What really struck me this week is how much of the conversation shifted from "what to eat" to "how to make it feel normal." Between the bread alternatives blowing up and all the social stuff I've been writing about lately (weird server looks, anyone?), it seems like people are settling into this lifestyle enough that they're solving the living with it problems, not just the surviving it problems. That's a shift.

I'm curious though, are we getting too complicated? Like, when we're blending eggs and chicken into tortilla shapes, are we making this harder than it needs to be, or is that just what sustainable eating looks like for most people? Genuinely asking.

Give Me 8 Minutes. I'll Save You 10+ Years Of Carnivore Mistakes.

Bart's hitting on something real here, and the comments are saying it loud: people are exhausted by the constant pivot-and-retry cycle. You see it everywhere in the community right now. ▶
🔥 Science Paul Saladino MD

You'll Never Eat Sardines Again

Paul's raising some legitimate concerns about canned sardines, but the community's got a point too. Heavy metals in small fish are genuinely less of an issue than in large predators, and modern canning standards (especially BPA-free options like Wild Planet) have actually improved. The real takeaway isn't "never eat sardines" but "pick your source carefully." Water or brine packed, minimal processing, and yes, grass-fed if you can find it. For most people eating sardines a few times a week, they're still one of the most nutrient-dense, affordable foods on the planet. Fear mongering doesn't help anyone make better decisions.

🔥🔥 Trending Coach Carnivore Cam

You Can Eat Bread And Still Be Healthy (Carnivore Bread)

This recipe is blowing up in the comments for good reason, but notice what people are actually saying: some crushed it, others found it knocked them out of ketosis. That's the real story here. Egg white powder and cottage cheese have carbs, and if you're deep in carnivore for metabolic reasons, this is a "sometimes food" at best, not a daily staple. The people celebrating the crispy crust and the camping-trip convenience are being honest about what this is, and that's worth respecting. If you're missing bread texture or you're building a reintroduction strategy, the recipe works. But if you're doing carnivore because carbs derail you, this isn't your answer.
