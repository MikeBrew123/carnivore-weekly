This Week's Roundup

- Chloe, Community Scout

Week of September 02, 2026

Okay so this week hit different. HomeSteadHow dropped a video called "Everybody Hates THIS about Carnivore" and it absolutely blew up, 45k views and the comments section turned into a full-on group therapy session. The frustration everyone's venting about? Watching people you love stay sick when you know this could help them. One comment from a 78-year-old guy married to a vegetarian really got me: he keeps a note on his bathroom mirror that just says "Let them." Five years carnivore, still happily married, and he's made peace with it. That's honestly the most mature take I've seen on this whole thing.

The thread was full of people saying the same stuff we've all felt, that ache of knowing someone would heal if they'd just try it, but also recognizing you can't drag anyone across the finish line. It's the weird loneliness of this way of eating, right? You feel amazing, you want to shout it from the rooftops, and then you watch someone reach for their third dinner roll while complaining about their joint pain. If you're newer and hitting that wall where beef sounds boring, I literally just wrote about this, you're not alone, and it passes.

Anyway, I'm sitting with this question: how do you balance sharing what's working for you without becoming that person at every family dinner? Curious what y'all have figured out.

The comment from the 78-year-old really hits it, though. "Let them" isn't just acceptance, it's wisdom. 🔥🔥 Mental Health Kent Carnivore

Black People Get Sick on Carnivore Diet?

The comment section here speaks louder than the video ever could. Black carnivores are sharing real results. reversed diabetes, cleared skin, joint pain gone, energy back. and they're not interested in debate. What gets me is how this conversation keeps circling back to the same myth: that carnivore is somehow incompatible with certain populations. History and lived experience both say otherwise. The Maasai, Samburu, and other pastoral cultures thrived on meat-based diets for centuries, and today's Black carnivores are doing the same. The real question isn't whether carnivore works across racial lines, it's why misinformation about nutrition still gets amplified in spaces where health disparities already run deep.

- Sarah

🔥 Recipe Shawn Baker MD

2 Million People Just Watched Carnivore "Wreck" His Bloodwork

The 30-day carnivore experiment has become the internet's favorite bloodwork drama, and I get why. One month isn't enough time to know much of anything. Your body needs weeks just to shift fuel sources, let alone rebuild tissues and stabilize hormones. The community comments here are telling the real story though, people saying three months, five years, and even 73-year-olds hitting their stride long after the initial adjustment phase. If someone's sluggish in week one, that's not failure, that's adaptation. The cholesterol spike everyone freaks out about? That's often your body finally getting the nutrients it needs to repair itself, not a red flag.
