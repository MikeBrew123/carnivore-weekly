This Week's Roundup

- Chloe, Community Scout

Week of April 06, 2026

Okay so the post that absolutely owned Reddit this week was from u/IM_DaWarez: 95 lbs gone, A1C from 7.4 to 4.1, off six medications, and he literally broke up with his doctor. His words: "My New Year's resolution was to limit my exposure to medical going forward." 388 upvotes. The comments were wild. People sharing their own medication exits, one person saying they dropped their doctor entirely and now order their own labs. Here's the thing, though. Across the whole sub this week, the transformation stories wouldn't stop. A 28-year-old guy lost 20 lbs in two months and the comments were all about how his physique is changing faster than the scale. A woman posted that her period came back after just 11 days on carnivore, and the replies turned into this incredible thread of women sharing hormone recovery stories. If you missed Sarah's piece on getting your electrolytes right, it's a perfect companion to what Dr. Tony Hampton dropped on YouTube this week about LMNT and Re-Lyte.

On the YouTube side, Keto Twins are everywhere right now. Their keto dessert video pulled 20K views and the comments turned into a recipe workshop, with people swapping cookie brands and sharing allulose hacks for ice cream that doesn't freeze rock-solid. Their eating out guide hit 16K views and honestly the comment section reads like a crowdsourced restaurant survival manual. People are ordering crustless pizza bowls, taco salads without the shell, and sushi rolls without rice. Meanwhile Kent Carnivore tackled the "low protein is healthiest" claim that's been floating around longevity circles, and Ground Beef Chief introduced his girlfriend at Week 74 of his journey. The comment "your heart rate was high cos you're in love" got 20 likes. That's the kind of content that makes you remember why this community is different.

The funniest post of the week? Someone complaining that the most annoying part of carnivore is that none of their clothes fit anymore. 236 upvotes. One person said they went from 2XL to XL, bought a whole wardrobe, then dropped to L, then M. The top comment: "Most expensive part of carnivore isn't the meat." And someone else buying a whole NY strip loin at Costco because they're done pretending individual steaks make financial sense. I love this community. So tell me, what's the most unexpected change you didn't plan for when you started?

🔥🔥 Trending Dr. Tony Hampton - The Metabolic Health Doc

What's Actually in Your Electrolyte Packets?

Dr. Tony Hampton does something really valuable here. He pulls apart the ingredient lists of LMNT, Re-Lyte, and Keto Chow side by side and asks the question most of us skip: what else are you swallowing besides sodium and potassium? The comments are telling. People sharing stories about maltodextrin hiding in "natural flavors," others landing in the ER from potassium overdoses, and plenty who ditched the packets entirely for DIY salt water and feel better. If you're spending $45 a month on electrolyte products, this is 22 minutes that could save you money and maybe a trip to urgent care.

How to Eat Keto at Any Restaurant

If you've ever panicked at a restaurant menu while your friends order pasta, this one's for you. 🔥🔥 Trending Kent Carnivore

The Low-Protein Myth Gets a Reality Check

Kent Carnivore tackles the claim that low-protein diets produce the best health outcomes. It's a claim that keeps resurfacing in vegan and fruitarian circles, and Kent breaks down why the studies behind it don't hold up when you look at body composition, muscle retention, and metabolic markers. The comment section is full of people sharing their own n=1 results, including one commenter doing 150 pullups at age 50 after three decades of high-protein eating. The protein debate matters because too many people are still scared of eating enough, and that fear keeps them underfed and losing muscle.

- Sarah
