This Week's Roundup

- Chloe, Community Scout

Week of September 13, 2026

Okay so this week the community was apparently hungry for. Cheese balls? Coach Carnivore Cam dropped a two-ingredient crispy parmesan cheese ball recipe and it absolutely took off, nearly 80k views and people in the comments are already debating whether to add rosemary or keep it pure. Someone also pointed out he's "so British it's delightful" which, honestly, fair. It's giving cozy carnivore comfort food energy, and I'm not mad about it. Sometimes you just need a crispy, gooey snack that doesn't derail everything.

Beyond the cheese ball moment, I noticed a lot of quieter conversations happening in DMs and smaller threads this week, people hitting that weird patch a few months in where things feel. Off. If that's you, Sarah actually wrote something really helpful about feeling suddenly sick after months of eating meat that's worth a read. It's one of those posts that makes you feel less alone when your body decides to get dramatic for no obvious reason.

Anyway, it feels like we're collectively settling into fall mode, comfort recipes, troubleshooting the unexpected, figuring out what actually works long-term versus what worked in the honeymoon phase. I'm curious: are you someone who thrives when things get cozy and routine, or does the seasonal shift throw you off?

New to carnivore? Our health coach Sarah put together a free email series, Your First Weeks on Carnivore, short notes covering what to eat, what to expect, and how to make it stick. Get it here →

🔥 Motivation Dr. Tony Hampton - The Metabolic Health Doc

Carnivore Diet Linked to HEART ATTACKS?

The comments section here is the real story. You've got people saying they reversed heart disease on carnivore, got zero CAC scores, resolved inflammation completely. Then you've got the flip side: the "80% carnivore" crowd who are probably still eating their kids' leftover pizza and wondering why they're not seeing results. The gap between strict adherence and casual label-wearing matters enormously when we're talking about heart health. What Dr. Hampton nails here is that one bad study or one elevated cholesterol number doesn't prove the diet kills you, just like one good number doesn't prove it saves you. You need the full picture: CAC scores, inflammation markers, actual artery imaging, not just LDL panic. The real takeaway for your health is stop letting one doctor's outdated playbook (statins for everyone) dictate your entire metabolic future.

- Marcus

▶
🔥 Recipe Jenny Mitich

What to Eat When Sugar Cravings Hit on Carnivore

The comments here reveal something I see constantly in coaching: cravings aren't actually about willpower, they're about satiety. Notice how the people who cracked it point to the same thing. fat. Butter on eggs, tallow, clarified butter on pork rinds. One commenter nailed it: "sugar cravings usually mean I haven't eaten enough fat." That's not an accident. The ones struggling are often the same people eating lean meat and skipping the fattier cuts. If you're still battling sweet cravings after a few weeks on carnivore, your macro balance is probably off. Check out our guide on macros for carnivore to see if you're actually hitting your fat targets. What also stands out is how many people mention sleep as a trigger. that person who said they crave carbs without good sleep isn't alone. Tired nervous systems reach for quick energy, and salt and electrolytes can genuinely help stabilize that impulse before it becomes a craving.

- Sarah
