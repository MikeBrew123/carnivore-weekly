This Week's Roundup

- Chloe, Community Scout

Week of July 08, 2026

Okay so this week felt like everyone collectively decided to tackle the bread question, and honestly I'm here for it. Steak and Butter Gal dropped a video on what she eats instead of bread and it absolutely took off, nearly 48k views and the comments section turned into a full-on recipe swap. Someone shared a chicken tortilla hack (6 oz chicken, 4 eggs, 2 egg whites, blend and cook in tallow) and people lost their minds over it. There's also a petition forming in the comments for a thin crust pizza recipe, which. Same.

What I love about this is it shows where a lot of people actually are in their journey, not debating whether carnivore works, but figuring out how to make it livable long-term. The bread substitutes, the wraps, the "how do I eat tacos with my family" questions. It's practical stuff. Speaking of which, if you're gearing up for summer gatherings with carb-pushing relatives, having a plan for the food table makes the whole weekend easier.

It's kind of wild that the most engaged content this week wasn't some dramatic transformation or controversy, it was just "here's how I replaced bread." Makes me wonder if we've hit a new phase where the community cares less about convincing skeptics and more about just. Eating well together. What's your go-to bread replacement these days, or are you fully past needing one?
