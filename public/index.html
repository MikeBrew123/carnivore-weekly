This Week's Roundup

- Chloe, Community Scout

Week of October 14, 2026

Okay so this week was basically a love letter to ground beef, and honestly I'm here for it. Dr. Tony Hampton dropped a video defending our beloved budget protein and it blew up, 37k views with comments full of people proudly declaring their daily ground beef habits. One person literally said "ground beef, bacon, and eggs" is their everyday meal and I felt so seen. It's giving "we don't need fancy cuts to thrive" energy, which is exactly what people need to hear when grocery bills are what they are right now.

The vibe in the community this week felt really practical and grounded (pun intended). Less "look at my perfect steak" content, more "here's how to actually make this work on a Tuesday when you're exhausted." Speaking of which, if you missed my easy dinner ideas for nights you genuinely cannot be bothered to cook, that post pairs nicely with this whole ground-beef-is-enough mood.

I'm genuinely curious though, has anyone else noticed the shift away from ribeye worship toward more realistic everyday eating content? Like are we finally past the phase where carnivore felt like it had to look expensive to be legitimate? Because I really hope so.

New to carnivore? Our health coach Sarah put together a free email series, Your First Weeks on Carnivore, short notes covering what to eat, what to expect, and how to make it stick. Get it here →

🔥 Success Story Carnivore Revolution

3 Carnivore Mistakes That Made Me Feel Worse!

The "worse before better" thing is so real, and I think this video nails why so many people bail too early. Most of the community comments here are pointing to the same culprit: salt and electrolytes. That person who went from 150mmHg blood pressure down to normal just by adding salt? That's not a coincidence. A lot of people start carnivore expecting their body to feel amazing immediately, but they're actually walking into a dehydration and electrolyte deficit without realizing it. Check out our electrolyte guide if you're dealing with fatigue or weird symptoms in week two. The other thing I'm noticing in these comments is patience, which honestly sounds like something we don't talk about enough. Your body isn't broken if it takes a month to adjust. Stick with it.

- Chloe

🔥 Success Story Carnivore Claire

6 Carnivore Habits That Destroy Love Handles & Make You Richer (140lbs Lost)

Claire nails something I see over and over in coaching: the people who stick with carnivore long-term aren't the ones obsessing over perfection, they're the ones who solve the practical problems first. Budget stress and meal boredom kill more diets than any macro miscalculation ever will. The crockpot cube steak trick, shopping on a full stomach, buying in bulk at Costco. these aren't sexy hacks, but they're exactly what makes the difference between "I tried carnivore for three weeks" and "I lost 140 pounds." When you remove friction from the diet, you remove the excuses that derail you. That's when real change happens.

- Sarah

Community's clearly hungry for more non-airfryer options too, so the accessibility angle here is smart. This is what wins over people who are skeptical, not arguments.

🔥 Recipe Dr. Tony Hampton - The Metabolic Health Doc

You Ate ZERO Carbs… Why Is Your Blood Sugar Rising?

Okay, this is the dawn phenomenon question that shows up in my coaching inbox at least once a week, usually from someone panicking at their CGM data. Dr. Hampton's explaining gluconeogenesis, but what I love about the comments here is that people are *getting it* now, not just the theory but how it actually plays out. Someone mentioned they know their body well enough to predict it, and that's the real skill, right? That's what happens when you stop fighting the process and start understanding your own metabolic rhythm. The fasting comments show people learning through their own data too, not just blindly trusting a number on the screen. This matters because so many people quit carnivore thinking their blood sugar control broke, when really it's just their liver doing exactly what it's supposed to do. If you're seeing elevated fasting glucose on carnivore, check out our CGM guide, it covers this exact scenario and why timing matters more than panic.
