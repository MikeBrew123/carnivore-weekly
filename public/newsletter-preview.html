What the Community Is Asking This Week
“I'm not a coffee drinker. Now that it's cold, I want something hot to sip on. What do I do?”
Bone broth is the answer so many carnivore folks land on, and it's genuinely satisfying in a way that nothing sweet can match. Warm, salty, rich fat on a cold morning hits a completely different spot than a sugary drink ever could. Many community members keep a thermos of it going all day once the weather turns.
“I keep trying to tell friends about carnivore because it helped me so much, but nobody wants to hear it. Anyone else?”
You're definitely not alone in this. Many people find that the most effective thing is just to look good and feel great and let curiosity do the talking. When a friend asks what you're doing differently, that's your opening, and it lands a lot better than an unsolicited pitch.
“How do I know if I'm eating too much fat? I'm a smaller person and I'm not sure where my numbers should be.”
Many people find that hunger is the most honest guide here, especially in the early weeks. If you're eating to satiety, not forcing fat, and your energy is stable, you're likely in a good place. If you're feeling sluggish or your digestion is off, those are the signals worth paying attention to.
Try This Week

Pick one meal this week and write down exactly what you ate and how you felt two hours later. That's it. No app, no spreadsheet required. Marcus says this single habit is what separates people who troubleshoot effectively from people who guess. One meal, one note, real data.
