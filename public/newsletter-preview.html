What's Trending

The protein-first food pyramid is here: A redesigned food pyramid that puts protein at the base went viral this week. Nutritionists are split, but the carnivore community is calling it a win. After decades of grain-heavy guidance, seeing meat and eggs at the foundation feels like a turning point.

Meatstock 2026 sold out: 1,600 people packed into one room to talk about meat. The lineup included Dr. Shawn Baker, Dr. Anthony Chaffee, and a live cooking demo that had the whole venue smelling like ribeye. If you missed it, Chloe's got a full recap dropping next week.

K-pop meets carnivore: A major K-pop star shared her all-meat meals on social media and the fan reaction was... a lot. The crossover nobody predicted is bringing a whole new audience to this way of eating, and the comment sections are genuinely entertaining.
Community Pulse

The coffee conversation exploded this week and I don't think it's settling down anytime soon. Some folks are reporting better sleep and steadier energy after dropping caffeine entirely. Others swear black coffee with a side of butter is non-negotiable. Here's what I find interesting: the research on caffeine and cortisol response varies a lot depending on your baseline stress levels and how long you've been adapted. It's not a simple yes or no answer, and that's exactly why we wrote a full breakdown for you this week. On another note, beef tallow keeps gaining ground as the cooking fat of choice. I've seen more people ditching seed oils for rendered fat in the last month than in the entire past year. The trend is real and the science backs it up.
