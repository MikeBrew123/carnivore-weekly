What the Community Is Asking This Week
“I've been on this for less than a week and only lost 3 pounds. Everyone online seems to drop 7 or 8 pounds right away. Am I doing something wrong?”
You're not doing anything wrong at all. Many people find that early weight loss varies a lot depending on how much water and glycogen your body was storing before you started. Sarah covers exactly this in 'Only Down 3 Pounds in Week One? Why That's Normal' this week, and her take might genuinely make you feel a lot better about where you are.
“People keep telling me that cutting out grains and eating this way will wreck my gut. But honestly, my digestion has never felt this good. What's going on?”
A lot of people in the community report the same thing, better digestion and less bloating once they remove processed foods and grains. The science on this is still evolving, so it's worth keeping your doctor in the loop, but your personal experience is worth paying attention to and tracking over time.
“I came back to carnivore after a year and a half away, and I feel dramatically better already after just one week. Why does it seem to work faster the second time around?”
Many returning carnivore eaters say the same thing, that the transition feels smoother and the benefits kick in faster the second time. One possible reason is that your body has done this before and adapts more quickly. It's also possible that having a year away gave you a very clear comparison point, so you notice the difference more sharply this time.
Try This Week

Pull out your keto staples and read the labels this week. Pick up any keto bread, wrap, or packaged product you've been eating regularly and look at the ingredient list. If you see modified wheat starch, oat fiber, or a long list of gums and fillers, that product might be worth setting aside for a couple of weeks to see if your results change. Just plain meat, fat, salt, and water for seven days is a clean test that tells you a lot.
