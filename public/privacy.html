Our Philosophy

We're here to help you navigate the carnivore lifestyle, not to hoard your data. We collect the bare minimum needed to make the site work and to keep the "Pro" tools running smoothly. We don't sell your personal info to third parties. Period.

Analytics & Google Signals

We use Google Analytics (GA4) with Google Signals enabled. This helps us understand how folks use the site across different devices (like starting on a phone and finishing on a laptop).

What Google sees: If you're signed into your Google account and have "Ads Personalization" turned on, Google may associate your visit with your account.

How to opt-out: You can manage your data via Google's My Activity or use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Payments & Stripe

When you upgrade to a "Pro" account, we use Stripe to handle the money stuff.

  • We never see or store your full credit card number. Stripe handles all the heavy lifting and security.
  • Stripe collects basic info (like your email and zip code) to prevent fraud and process your subscription.

Data We Collect

We collect data in several ways when you use the site. Here is a full accounting of what we collect, why, and how long we keep it.

Calculator Usage Data: When you use the free or paid calculator, we collect the inputs you provide (such as age, sex, height, weight, activity level, dietary goals, and diet type) along with pseudonymous technical data including your referral source, UTM campaign parameters, landing page URL, device type, browser analytics identifiers (such as the Google Analytics client ID from cookies), and IP-derived location data. This data is stored on our servers and used for aggregate product analytics, funnel performance analysis, and service improvement. We retain this data for up to 26 months for analytical purposes. This data is not linked to your name or email address unless you make a purchase, at which point your calculator inputs may be associated with your transaction record.

Purchase and Transaction Data: When you purchase a report, we store your email address, calculator inputs, and payment confirmation details. Report access is time-limited (48 hours). You can save or email yourself the report before it expires. We do not maintain user accounts, login credentials, or ongoing personal data archives. Transaction records (including email, amount paid, and payment status) may be retained indefinitely for accounting, tax, and dispute resolution purposes.

Payment Processing: All payment processing is handled by Stripe, Inc. We never see, access, or store your full credit card number, CVV, or banking details. Stripe collects information necessary to process your payment and prevent fraud, subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy. We receive only confirmation of payment status (completed, refunded, or failed).

Cookies and Analytics: We use Google Analytics (GA4) cookies to understand site usage. Third-party affiliate partners (such as Amazon, ButcherBox, and LMNT) may set their own cookies when you click affiliate links. We use server-side analytics events for purchase and refund tracking to ensure accurate reporting regardless of browser settings.

Infrastructure Data: We use Cloudflare for site delivery and security. Cloudflare may process technical data (IP address, browser headers) to protect against attacks and ensure service availability.

How We Use Your Data

  • To calculate and deliver your personalized macro results and reports
  • To process payments and handle refunds
  • To analyze aggregate usage patterns and improve the calculator and site
  • To understand which traffic sources and campaigns drive engagement
  • To prevent fraud and abuse

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

AI-Assisted Content

Carnivore Weekly uses a combination of AI synthesis and human curation. All content on this site, including blog posts, editorial commentary, and paid reports, is human-directed and AI-assisted. A human editorial team guides topics, reviews output, and makes final decisions. AI language models (including Claude by Anthropic) assist with research synthesis, content drafting, and report generation.

For paid reports specifically: your calculator inputs are sent to the AI provider solely for the purpose of generating your personalized report. We do not send payment information, email addresses, or other identifying data to AI models. The resulting report is AI-generated content reviewed under human editorial oversight.
