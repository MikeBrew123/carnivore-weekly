Carnivore Coach

A 12-week guided program for people who know what to eat but keep falling off. Your macros, recalculated as you lose. A different focus each week. And a coach in your inbox who answers when the wheels wobble: day-5 headaches, restaurant menus, family dinners, the 8pm cravings.

How the 12 weeks run

Each week has one job. You start with a baseline (and a conversation with your doctor, especially if you take medications, that part isn't optional with us). Then: adaptation and electrolytes, energy and sleep, your protein floor, eating out and travel, evening cravings, a mid-point check on your progress, body composition, social pressure, budget meal systems, maintenance macros, and finally a graduation plan so week 13 doesn't undo week 12.

What's included

  • Weekly check-ins that track how you feel, not just what you weigh: energy, joints, sleep, brain fog
  • Macros that update as your body changes, so you're never running on stale numbers
  • Real answers to your questions, usually within a day, from a coach team that knows your history
  • Travel and eating-out playbooks, because life doesn't pause for 12 weeks

Being straight with you: most answers are drafted by AI trained on our coaching approach, and reviewed with human oversight. That's how we keep this affordable. It's warm, it's personal to your data, and a real person is watching. Nothing here is medical advice, and members on medications are asked to involve their doctor from day one.
