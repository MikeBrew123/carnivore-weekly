Hey,

Four weeks ago, this was an experiment. You weren't sure you'd last the first weekend. Now you've been through the rough patch, learned to salt like you mean it, figured out restaurants, sorted your electrolytes, and stocked a freezer. That's not a diet attempt anymore. That's a skill set.

So before anything else, take a real inventory. Not the scale. The other stuff:

Your 4-week check-in questions:

  • Is your energy steadier through the afternoon than it was a month ago?
  • Are the cravings quieter? Not gone, maybe, but quieter?
  • How are you sleeping? How do your clothes fit? How's the bloating?
  • And the big one: does eating feel simpler than it did four weeks ago?

Everyone's answers will be different, and I won't pretend everybody gets the same results by week 4. Some changes show up early, some take months. But if even two of those answers moved in the right direction, you have real evidence this is working for your body. Write it down somewhere. Future you will want it.
