Hey,

Two full weeks. That's officially past the adaptation hump, and if you've been salting like I told you on Day 10, you're probably starting to feel the payoff. So today I want to talk about something small that makes a surprisingly big difference: what's hanging on your fridge.

Here's a pattern I've seen over and over. People save screenshots of food lists and recipes to their phone, and then never look at them again. Because at 5 PM, when you're tired and hungry and staring into the fridge, you're not going to unlock your phone, scroll through your camera roll, and squint at a screenshot. You're going to grab whatever's easiest.

Printed things on the fridge beat screenshots. Every time. The information is just there, at eye level, exactly when you're making the decision. No willpower required. This is the same reason meal preppers cook on Sundays. You make the good choice once, in advance, instead of fifty times a week.

What's actually on my fridge:

  • A one-page food list. What's a yes, what's a no, no thinking required.
  • Two or three recipe cards for the meals I make on repeat.
  • This week's simple meal plan, scribbled on the back of an envelope. Fancy isn't the point. Visible is the point.
