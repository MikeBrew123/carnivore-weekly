The honest timeline, then yours

Hi, it's Sarah.

Somewhere around day 5 the question changes. You're past just getting through it, and now you want to know when this starts feeling good. I promised you a straight answer yesterday, so here it is.

What usually improves, and roughly when

  • This week: Appetite starts to settle and you get your first long stretches without cravings. Energy is still up and down, and sleep can wobble. Both are normal.
  • Week 2: Energy evens out. The 3pm slump softens or disappears. Cravings get quieter.
  • Weeks 3 to 4: A clearer head, hunger that feels different, and for a lot of people, better sleep.

Some people feel it by day 4. Others need three weeks or more. What you ate before, how you're sleeping, your age and any medications all move the clock, and a slow start says nothing about how well keto will work for you. If you're several weeks in and still wiped out, that's usually fixable. I wrote a checklist for exactly that: still exhausted after adaptation.

Notice what's not on that list

The scale. The first few pounds are mostly water, and after that, weight tends to come off in steps with flat stretches in between. Feeling better is the early signal. The number is the slow one.

Which is why the next question is always the same: how long is my goal going to take?
