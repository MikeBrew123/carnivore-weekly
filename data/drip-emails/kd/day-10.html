Cook once, eat all week

Marcus here again. You're ten days in, which means you've outlasted the hardest stretch. Nice work. Now the two things most likely to knock you off keto are money and time, so today we're fixing both in one afternoon.

The budget side

Keto has a reputation for being expensive, and that reputation is mostly earned by keto-branded products. The bars, the shakes, the special breads. Skip them. With those, you're paying for the word on the label. Real food is the affordable version of this diet.

Where the money goes furthest

  • Eggs. Still one of the cheapest proteins you can buy.
  • Cheaper cuts. Chicken thighs over breasts, chuck roast over steak, pork shoulder over chops. More flavor anyway.
  • Ground beef in bulk. Portion it, freeze it, thaw as you go.
  • Frozen vegetables and canned fish. They cost less and keep for months.
  • Whatever protein is on sale this week. Build the week around the deal, not the recipe.

If you want this fully mapped out, the blog has a complete week of keto meal prep for under $50, and an even leaner thirty dollar keto week if things are tight this month.

The time side

Meal prep doesn't need to be six hours and forty matching containers. Here's the version that survives a real week:

The one-hour Sunday

  • Pick two proteins and cook all of it at once. Sheet pan in the oven, ground beef on the stove.
  • Roast two trays of low-carb vegetables while the proteins cook.
  • Hard-boil a dozen eggs for grab-and-go.
  • Box five lunches before anything goes into the fridge. That step is the whole game.

Why box the lunches first? Because you don't fail keto at the grocery store. You fail it at 6 pm, tired, staring into a fridge with no plan. Prep removes the decision, and removing decisions is the entire trick. Is eating the same lunch all week a little boring? Sure. Boring is what working looks like in week two.

One tweak makes all of this sharper: portion to your numbers. If you haven't run the free calculator yet, it takes about 3 minutes, and then you'll know how much protein belongs in each of those boxes instead of eyeballing it.

Cook once. Eat all week. Spend less. That's the protocol, and it's simpler than whatever you were doing before keto.
