You built something real

Twenty-eight days ago you signed up for this, probably somewhere between hopeful and skeptical. Today's the last email of the program, so let's be honest about what just happened.

You built something real.

Not a streak. Streaks break the first weekend something goes sideways. What you built is a skill set. You know how to get through the rough adaptation days. You can order at a restaurant without a second thought. You've told the pie person "no thanks" and lived. You've hit a stall, or you will, and you know it doesn't mean anything's broken.

Four weeks proves something specific: this isn't a thing you tried, it's a thing you can do. There's a difference. Most people who quit keto quit in the first two weeks, right where the hard part lives. You went through all of it, the flu, the wall, the family dinners, and you're still here. That wasn't luck and it wasn't willpower. You learned how this works, one week at a time.

And nobody can unlearn that for you. Whatever the next few months look like, you now know exactly how to come back to this, because you've already done it once from zero.
