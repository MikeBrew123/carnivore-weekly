Welcome to the wall

Chloe again. Sarah and Marcus got you through week one. I get the less glamorous part: week two.

Here's what I see over and over in the community. Week one is exciting. The scale moves, everything's new, you feel like you cracked a code. Then week two shows up with none of that. The scale sits still, you're bored of eggs, and a little voice starts asking if this is even working.

That's the wall. Almost everyone hits it. The people who make it to month two aren't the ones who never hit it. They're the ones who knew it was coming.

The stall

That first-week drop was real, but a lot of it was water. When you cut carbs, your body lets go of stored water fast, which makes for a thrilling first week on the scale. Fat loss is slower, and it's not a straight line. People constantly report the scale freezing for a week or two and then dropping again like nothing happened.

So before you change anything, wait it out and measure more than one thing. Waist, energy, how your clothes fit, how you're sleeping. If the scale's still stuck in another week or two, run the stall checklist. It's usually one of a handful of fixable things.

And if you've been eyeballing portions since day one, this is a good week to get real numbers. The free macro calculator takes about three minutes. A lot of stalls turn out to be a portion problem wearing a mystery costume.

The boredom

By day 14, most people are rotating the same four meals. That's efficient, right up until it makes you want to eat a sandwich out of pure spite.

The fix isn't a whole new plan. It's one new meal a week. That's it. There are 70+ keto recipes on the site with the macros already worked out, so pick whatever sounds good and skip the math.

The doubt

"Is this even working?" is the most common week-two question I see, and it almost always shows up during a stall. Notice that it's a feeling, not a measurement. So measure.

Signs it's working that aren't the scale

  • Steadier energy through the afternoon, no 3pm crash
  • Going longer between meals without white-knuckling it
  • Less bloat, clothes fitting a little differently
  • Fewer cravings ambushing you at night
  • Sleeping more solidly than you were two weeks ago

If even a couple of those are happening, it's working. The scale is just the slowest, moodiest way to track it.

How people actually get through this week

Not with motivation. Motivation is what got you to day one. What gets people to day 30 is smaller and more boring: don't renegotiate your diet at 9pm when you're tired and snacky. Keep dinners planned so there's no decision to make. Give the scale a week off if it's messing with your head.

You're not doing anything wrong. You're just in the middle. The middle always feels like this, and it passes.
