Stop guessing. Start counting.

Hey, Marcus here. I'm the performance coach on the KetoDial team. Sarah covers the health side, I cover numbers and systems, and she's handing me the mic for today.

Here's the pattern I've seen over and over in ten years of coaching: most people don't quit keto because it's too hard. They quit because it quietly stops working. And it stops working because they're guessing.

Guessing looks like this. You skip the bread, order the burger without the bun, and figure you're "pretty low carb." Then the scale won't move, the energy never shows up, and you decide keto doesn't work for you. Meanwhile a splash of sauce here, a handful of nuts there, and a "low carb" wrap that isn't have been nudging you over your limit all week. You can blow your carb budget without eating a single slice of bread.

The math doesn't lie. Keto works inside a carb range, and you can't stay inside a range you've never measured. So today has two jobs.

Job 1: Know your numbers

You need three: a daily carb cap, a protein target, and a fat range. They come from your weight, your activity, and your goal. Generic advice hands everyone the same number, but a 150-pound woman who walks every day and a 230-pound guy doing physical work don't have the same numbers.

The KetoDial calculator works yours out free in about 3 minutes, no email needed. And if you ever want the full plan built from those numbers, there are paid reports too, things like a 7-day meal plan and a doctor's report. No pressure either way. The free numbers alone put you ahead of most people doing keto.

Job 2: Shop with a strategy

Your numbers only matter if the right food is in the house. Here's how I'd run your next grocery trip:

Your grocery strategy

  • Shop the outer walls first. Meat, eggs, cheese, and low-carb vegetables live there.
  • Read labels in the middle aisles. Sauces, dressings, and "keto" snacks hide carbs.
  • Buy proteins you'll actually cook on a tired Tuesday, not just the ambitious ones.
  • Remember this: if it never makes it into your cart, it can't wreck your week at 9 pm.

If you want the full list to work from, there's a complete keto grocery list on the blog. And if keto has beaten you before, why you quit keto last time is worth five minutes. Guessing shows up in that story too.

That's the whole assignment. Get your numbers, shop the list. Systems beat willpower every single time.
