What's happening inside you right now

Your body spent years running mostly on sugar. This week, it started retooling to run on fat instead. That's fat adaptation, and it's the reason the water weight moved, the reason you needed the extra salt, and the reason your hunger is starting to behave differently.

Here's the honest version: one week means adaptation is underway, not finished. Weeks two through four are where the payoff concentrates, which is exactly why quitting now would be the worst-timed exit possible. The expensive part is behind you. The good part is ahead.
