Four weeks. Look at you.

Hey,

Four weeks ago, this was an experiment. Now you've been through the rough patch, learned to salt like you mean it, sorted your electrolytes, and figured out how to feed yourself without a single glance at the snack aisle. That's not a diet attempt anymore. That's a skill set.

Before anything else, take stock. Not the scale. The other stuff:

Your 4-week check-in questions:

  • Is your energy steadier through the afternoon than it was a month ago?
  • Are the cravings quieter? Not gone, maybe, but quieter?
  • How are you sleeping? How do your clothes fit? How's the bloating?
  • And the big one: does eating feel simpler than it did four weeks ago?

Everyone's answers will be different, and I won't pretend everybody gets the same results by week 4. But if even two of those moved in the right direction, you've got real evidence this is working for your body. Write it down. Future you will want it.
