Your First Weeks on Carnivore

Four things make a week work.

It's Marcus. I write the practical side of Carnivore Weekly: shopping, prep, planning.

Sarah warned you yesterday that day 3 is where this gets real. She's right, and the reason is usually logistics rather than willpower. Almost nobody quits because they can't cook. They quit because it's 6pm, there's nothing in the fridge that fits the plan, and the easiest thing in the house is a sandwich.

Most people reading this have run a plan before. Atkins, keto, paleo, points, an app that counted something. So I'll skip the pep talk. The difference this time is that there's very little to learn and quite a lot riding on buying the right things once.

Here's the shop.

The week-one shop

Quantities are one person, seven days. Feeding two, double the beef and the eggs. The salt lasts either way.

  • Ground beef, 80/20. 3 lb. Not lean. The fat is the fuel, and 93/7 leaves you hungry at 9pm.
  • Eggs. 18. The fastest meal you own. Three eggs in butter is breakfast in four minutes.
  • Butter. 1 lb. Salted is fine. It goes on the beef, in the eggs, in the pan.
  • Salt. One box. Coarse sea salt. When the carbs drop this fast most people need more of it than they're used to, and it's doing a job. If you've been told to watch your sodium, that instruction beats mine.

That's the whole list. Those four will carry you to day 7 on their own.

If you want more than that, in the order I'd add it:

  • Two steaks. Ribeye, sirloin, whatever the counter has marked down. Cook them on the two days you'd normally give up.
  • Bacon, 1 lb. Shortest ingredient list on the shelf wins. Most of them have sugar in the cure.
  • Half a pound of liver. Optional, and the most nutrient-dense thing in the case. If you don't like it, cube it small and cook it into the beef.

One cook, four lunches

Put 2 lb of the ground beef in your biggest pan. Medium-high, about 12 minutes, break it up as it browns. Season it to taste, and unless you've been told to watch your sodium, be less shy with the salt than you were a week ago. Covered dish, into the fridge.

That's four lunches that reheat in 90 seconds with butter on top. No recipe, no measuring, no deciding anything at 6pm. Do it on whichever day is quietest for you, not necessarily Sunday.

If 3 lb is more than you'll get through, split it into meal-sized portions and freeze them the day you buy it. Frozen on day one beats grey on day four.

Three things that look like they belong here and don't

  • Jerky. Nearly always sugar in the marinade. Read the back of the packet.
  • Pork rinds. Technically fine, practically a snack food. They're the easiest way to eat when you aren't hungry, and not eating when you aren't hungry is half of why this works.
  • Anything labelled “carnivore-friendly.” That's a price premium on a sticker. Beef, eggs, butter and salt don't need branding.

If you'd rather have the full chart on paper, the whole eat, limit and avoid picture rather than just week one, we sell it as a printable and most people put it on the fridge door. Entirely optional. The four lines above get you through the week either way.
