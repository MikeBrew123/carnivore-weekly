## Recipes 1-10 (Batch 1)

### 1. Chocolate Peanut Butter Fat Bombs
**Ingredients:** 2 oz cream cheese (softened), 2 tbsp natural peanut butter, 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder, 2 tbsp powdered erythritol, 1/2 tsp vanilla extract, pinch of sea salt
**Instructions:** 1) Mix cream cheese, peanut butter, cocoa, sweetener, vanilla, and salt until smooth. 2) Roll into 4 balls and freeze for 20 minutes before serving.
**Net Carbs:** 3.4g | **Calories:** 140 | **Fat:** 12.7g | **Protein:** 4.0g | **Prep:** 10 min | **Tag:** Chocolate Fix

### 2. No-Bake Lemon Cheesecake Cups
**Ingredients:** 4 oz cream cheese (softened), 1/4 cup heavy cream, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tbsp almond flour, 2 tbsp powdered erythritol, 1/2 tsp lemon zest
**Instructions:** 1) Beat cream cheese, heavy cream, lemon juice, sweetener, and zest until fluffy. 2) Sprinkle almond flour into 4 small cups, spoon filling on top, and chill.
**Net Carbs:** 4.1g | **Calories:** 244 | **Fat:** 24.3g | **Protein:** 4.2g | **Prep:** 10 min | **Tag:** Tangy & Creamy

### 3. Cinnamon Mug Cake
**Ingredients:** 1/4 cup almond flour, 1 egg, 1 tbsp melted butter, 1 tbsp erythritol, 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp baking powder, splash of vanilla extract
**Instructions:** 1) Stir all ingredients in a mug until smooth. 2) Microwave 60-75 seconds, then let cool for 1 minute.
**Net Carbs:** 4.0g | **Calories:** 339 | **Fat:** 30.5g | **Protein:** 12.1g | **Prep:** 5 min | **Tag:** Warm Bakery

### 4. Strawberry Cream Parfait
**Ingredients:** 1/2 cup sliced strawberries, 1/4 cup heavy cream, 2 oz cream cheese, 1 tbsp chia seeds, 1 tbsp erythritol, 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
**Instructions:** 1) Whip cream cheese, heavy cream, sweetener, and vanilla until light. 2) Layer with strawberries and chia seeds in 2 cups.
**Net Carbs:** 4.4g | **Calories:** 216 | **Fat:** 20.6g | **Protein:** 3.2g | **Prep:** 10 min | **Tag:** Fruity & Fresh

### 5. Chocolate Avocado Mousse
**Ingredients:** 1 ripe avocado, 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder, 3 tbsp coconut cream, 2 tbsp powdered erythritol, 1/2 tsp vanilla extract, pinch of salt
**Instructions:** 1) Blend all ingredients until completely smooth. 2) Chill for 15 minutes and serve in 2 portions.
**Net Carbs:** 2.9g | **Calories:** 172 | **Fat:** 15.2g | **Protein:** 3.0g | **Prep:** 5 min | **Tag:** Rich Chocolate

### 6. Almond Butter Cookie Bites
**Ingredients:** 2 tbsp almond butter, 1 tbsp almond flour, 1 egg yolk, 1 tbsp erythritol, 1/4 tsp vanilla extract, pinch of salt
**Instructions:** 1) Mix ingredients into a soft dough and form 6 mini bites. 2) Bake at 350°F for 8-10 minutes, then cool before serving.
**Net Carbs:** 1.8g | **Calories:** 112 | **Fat:** 9.8g | **Protein:** 4.2g | **Prep:** 12 min | **Tag:** Cookie Craving

### 7. Coconut Lime Chia Pudding
**Ingredients:** 1/3 cup coconut cream, 1 tbsp chia seeds, 1 tbsp lime juice, 1 tbsp unsweetened shredded coconut, 1 tbsp erythritol, lime zest (optional)
**Instructions:** 1) Stir coconut cream, chia, lime juice, coconut, sweetener, and zest together. 2) Chill for at least 30 minutes, then divide into 2 servings.
**Net Carbs:** 4.2g | **Calories:** 214 | **Fat:** 20.1g | **Protein:** 2.9g | **Prep:** 5 min | **Tag:** Tropical Sweet

### 8. Pumpkin Spice Cheesecake Mousse
**Ingredients:** 3 oz cream cheese, 3 tbsp pumpkin puree, 3 tbsp heavy cream, 1 tbsp erythritol, 1/2 tsp pumpkin pie spice, 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
**Instructions:** 1) Beat cream cheese, pumpkin, cream, sweetener, spice, and vanilla until smooth. 2) Spoon into 2 cups and chill before serving.
**Net Carbs:** 4.7g | **Calories:** 215 | **Fat:** 20.9g | **Protein:** 3.1g | **Prep:** 10 min | **Tag:** Fall Dessert

### 9. Raspberry Chocolate Bark
**Ingredients:** 1 oz sugar-free dark chocolate, 1 1/2 tsp coconut oil, 1/4 cup raspberries, 1 tbsp chopped almonds, pinch of sea salt
**Instructions:** 1) Melt chocolate with coconut oil, then spread thin on parchment. 2) Top with raspberries, almonds, and salt; freeze until firm and break into pieces.

### 10. Vanilla Pecan Ice Cream Bites
**Ingredients:** 1/3 cup heavy cream, 1 1/2 oz cream cheese, 2 tbsp chopped pecans, 1 tbsp erythritol, 1/2 tsp vanilla extract, pinch of salt
**Instructions:** 1) Blend cream, cream cheese, sweetener, vanilla, and salt until smooth. 2) Stir in pecans, spoon into 6 mini molds, and freeze until firm.

## BONUS PAGE 2: Sweetener Conversion Chart

| Sugar Amount | Erythritol | Monk Fruit (1:1 blend) | Pure Stevia | Allulose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 tsp | 1¼-1⅓ tsp | 1 tsp | Tiny pinch / few drops | 1⅓ tsp |
| 1 tbsp | 1 tbsp + 1 tsp | 1 tbsp | ~⅛ tsp powder / 6-9 drops | 1 tbsp + 1 tsp |
| ¼ cup | ⅓ cup | ¼ cup | ~¼ tsp powder / 18-24 drops | ⅓ cup |
| ½ cup | ⅔ cup | ½ cup | ~½ tsp powder / 36-48 drops | ⅔ cup |
| 1 cup | 1⅓ cups | 1 cup | ~1 tsp powder / 1½-2 tsp liquid | 1⅓ cups |

**Quick Notes:**
- Best for baking: allulose or monk fruit blend
- Best for no-bake desserts: powdered erythritol, monk fruit, or allulose
- Best for drinks and sauces: liquid stevia or allulose
- Least gritty: allulose
- Most likely to feel cooling: erythritol
- Best caramelization: allulose
