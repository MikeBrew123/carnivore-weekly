# Keto Dessert Recipe Cards — Sarah's Backstories

**1. Chocolate Peanut Butter Fat Bombs**
I made these the first week I went keto because I was convinced I'd never enjoy dessert again. Wrong. These little things taste like a Reese's cup that actually loves you back. They've been in my freezer rotation for three years straight and I don't see that changing.

**2. No-Bake Lemon Cheesecake Cups**
My friend brought these to a potluck in Whistler and I genuinely didn't believe they were keto. I made her text me the recipe before I left. They're tangy, creamy, and they take maybe ten minutes to throw together. Summer in a cup.

**3. Cinnamon Mug Cake**
This is my 9 PM "I need something warm and sweet right now" recipe. Two minutes in the microwave and it comes out like a little cinnamon cloud. I've made this more times than I'd care to admit, and it hits every single time.

**4. Strawberry Cream Parfait**
I built this one for a client who missed her morning yogurt parfaits. She texted me a photo the next day with about twelve heart emojis. It's simple, it's pretty, and it tastes like you're cheating even though you're not.

**5. Chocolate Avocado Mousse**
I know, I know. Avocado in dessert sounds weird. I thought so too until I actually tried it. The texture is unreal, and you genuinely can't taste the avocado. This one converts skeptics every time I serve it.

**6. Almond Butter Cookie Bites**
These remind me of the peanut butter cookies my mom used to make, just without the sugar crash afterward. Three ingredients, no baking required, and they disappear from the counter in about an hour. I keep a batch in the fridge at all times.

**7. Coconut Lime Chia Pudding**
I started making this during a July heat wave when I wanted something cold and tropical but didn't want to blow my macros. It tastes like a vacation. I prep four jars on Sunday and grab one whenever I need a pick-me-up.

**8. Pumpkin Spice Cheesecake Mousse**
Yes, I'm a pumpkin spice person. No shame. This mousse is what I make every October instead of ordering a latte full of sugar. It's rich, it's spiced perfectly, and it scratches that fall craving without any of the junk.

**9. Raspberry Chocolate Bark**
I developed this for a holiday gift exchange because I was tired of showing up empty-handed while everyone else brought fudge. People had no idea it was keto. I've been asked for this recipe more than any other one I make.

**10. Vanilla Pecan Ice Cream Bites**
These came from an accident, honestly. I was trying to make ice cream and it froze too hard, so I cut it into bites instead. Turns out that's the better format. Creamy vanilla, crunchy pecans, perfect portion size. Happy accidents.

**11. Fudgy Almond Flour Brownie Bites**
I spent an embarrassing amount of time getting these right. Six batches before the texture was fudgy enough. But now they're perfect. Dense, chocolatey, and they don't crumble apart like so many keto brownies do. Worth every failed attempt.

**12. Chocolate Avocado Mousse Cups**
This is the dressed-up version of my regular avocado mousse for when I want to feel fancy. Little cups, a dollop of whipped cream, maybe some cacao nibs on top. It takes five extra minutes and suddenly it's a dinner party dessert.

**13. Strawberry Cream Cheese Freezer Pops**
I made these for my neighbor's kids last summer and they had no idea they were eating something healthy. The parents asked me for the recipe that same afternoon. They're creamy, fruity, and they beat any store-bought popsicle I've tried.

**14. No-Bake Peanut Butter Coconut Bars**
These live in my freezer for hiking days. I grab two on my way out the door in Whistler and they keep me fueled for hours. Chewy, nutty, sweet enough to feel like a treat but solid enough to call it fuel.

**15. Mini Vanilla Cheesecake Jars**
I started making these in mason jars so I could meal-prep dessert. Which sounds ridiculous, but it changed my week. Grab a jar, eat a perfect little cheesecake at my desk. It's the small things that keep you consistent.

**16. Cinnamon Ricotta Dessert Bowl**
This one came from an Italian cooking class I took years ago. The instructor used sugar, obviously. I swapped in monk fruit and added cinnamon, and honestly it's better than the original. Five minutes, one bowl, total comfort.

**17. Lemon Chia Cream Pudding**
I eat this for breakfast more than I eat it for dessert, if I'm being honest. It's light, lemony, and the chia gives it this tapioca-like texture that I love. Make it the night before and it's waiting for you in the morning.

**18. Salted Caramel Pecan Fat Bombs**
These are dangerous. In the best way. The salt-sweet combination is addictive, and the pecans give them a praline vibe. I have to portion these out immediately or I'll eat the whole batch standing at the counter.

**19. Coconut Lime Ice Cream**
I was craving key lime pie on a random Tuesday and threw this together from what I had in the kitchen. It turned out better than any plan I could've made. Creamy coconut base, bright lime, and it scoops perfectly straight from the freezer.

**20. Mocha Whipped Cream Parfait**
Coffee and chocolate together is my love language. I layer this in a glass so it looks like something from a cafe, but it takes three minutes to make at home. It's my Friday night treat, and I look forward to it all week.

**21. Dark Chocolate Coconut Clusters**
I keep these in a jar on my desk for the 3 PM slump. Two clusters and a cup of tea, and I'm good for the rest of the afternoon. They're crunchy, chocolatey, and they satisfy that "I need something sweet" feeling instantly.

**22. Peanut Butter Mug Cheesecake**
A client told me about this concept, and I thought it sounded too good to be true. It's not. Creamy peanut butter cheesecake in a mug, ready in under five minutes. It's become my go-to when I don't want to make a whole dessert.

**23. Blueberry Cream Cheese Icebox Bites**
These remind me of the icebox cake my grandmother used to make at her lake house. I adapted it to be keto-friendly and cut it into little bites so I can grab just one or two. The blueberries make them feel summery and fresh.

**24. Almond Joy Fat Bomb Cups**
If you loved Almond Joy candy bars growing up, these are going to make your day. Coconut, almonds, dark chocolate. I don't even like coconut that much and I still eat these constantly. They're that good.

**25. Vanilla Mascarpone Berry Bowl**
This is the recipe I make when someone tells me keto desserts can't be elegant. Mascarpone is so silky and rich, and the berries add just enough brightness. I served this at a dinner party last month and two people asked if I'd made it from scratch. I had, and it took me four minutes.
