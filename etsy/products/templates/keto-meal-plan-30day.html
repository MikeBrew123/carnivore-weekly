30-Day Keto
Meal Plan

Simple. Repeatable. Results.

This plan is for keto beginners who want a system, not a science lecture. The target is simple: stay under 20g net carbs a day. That's it. No calorie counting. No weighing food. Repeatable meals you'll actually cook on a Tuesday night.

How to use it: Eat what's listed. Eat until full. Swap proteins if your store is out of something. Cook once, eat twice. Leftover days are built in on purpose.

Step-by-step versions of many of these recipes are free at ketodial.com, our keto site.

Weekly Prep Tips
  • Sunday batch cook: Make a dozen bacon spinach egg cups and a pan of egg casserole. Breakfasts are done for the week.
  • Boil a dozen eggs. Egg salad, tuna salad, and grab-and-go snacks all come from the same pot.
  • Clear the kitchen. Bread, pasta, rice, chips, sugar. Out of the house or out of sight. Willpower is a terrible plan.
  • Read labels. Net carbs = total carbs minus fiber. Sauces and dressings are where hidden carbs live.
  • Eat until full. Don't count calories this week. Your only job is staying under 20g net carbs.

Week 2: Adaptation

Days 8-14

This is keto flu week for most people. It's not the flu. It's electrolytes. Salt your food, drink water, and follow the plan. It passes.

Weekly Prep Tips
  • Electrolytes first: Headache, fatigue, brain fog? That's sodium, not sickness. Salt everything. Sip salted water or broth daily.
  • Magnesium and potassium: Avocado, spinach, and a magnesium supplement at night cover most of it. Leg cramps mean you're behind.
  • Sunday ground beef: Brown 2-3 lbs. It becomes taco meat, egg roll slaw, and salad topper all week.
  • Chaffles freeze great. Make a double batch, freeze, toast in the morning. Faster than cereal ever was.
  • Don't quit on day 10. Almost everyone who fails keto quits during this week. You're one salty broth away from feeling fine.

Weekly Prep Tips
  • Restaurant rules: Protein + vegetables + fat. Swap fries for salad. Ask for butter. Skip the bread basket. Done.
  • Sauce check: BBQ sauce, ketchup, and teriyaki are sugar bombs. Ranch, blue cheese, oil and vinegar are safe.
  • Bake once, eat twice: Frittata and quiche both give you two breakfasts per pan. Muffins keep 5 days in the fridge.
  • Slow cooker stew: Beef cabbage stew takes 10 minutes of work. Dinner tonight, lunch tomorrow.
  • Notice your hunger. By now you might skip a meal without thinking about it. That's fat adaptation working. Don't force three meals.

Weekly Prep Tips
  • Build your rotation. Pick your 5 favorite dinners from this month. Write them down. That's your permanent meal plan.
  • Sunday system: One pan of egg cups or casserole, one batch of ground beef, one soup or slow cooker meal. 90 minutes covers most of your week.
  • Stock the freezer. Chaffles, muffins, soup portions, cooked ground beef. A stocked freezer beats a weak moment every time.
  • Day 30 audit: Energy, sleep, mood, waistline, cravings. Write it all down. This is your baseline for the next 30.
  • What's next: Keep going. The plan doesn't end at 30 days. It starts there.

The Whole Protocol in One Line

Stay under 20g net carbs. Salt your food. Cook once, eat twice. Repeat for 30 days. Measure what matters and adjust based on data, not opinions.
