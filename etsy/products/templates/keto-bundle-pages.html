⚡ Electrolyte & Keto Flu Checklist

YOUR FIRST 2 WEEKS • PREVENT & FIX COMMON SYMPTOMS
🤒 Common Keto Flu Symptoms
Headache
Fatigue / brain fog
Muscle cramps
Dizziness
Nausea
Irritability
Sugar cravings
Trouble sleeping
Constipation
Heart palpitations
Most symptoms peak days 2-5 and resolve within 1-2 weeks. Electrolytes help a lot.
🎯 Daily Electrolyte Targets
ElectrolyteDaily Target
Sodium3,000 – 5,000 mg
Potassium3,500 – 4,700 mg
Magnesium300 – 400 mg
Food Sources
🧂Sodium: salt, bone broth, pickles, olives
🥑Potassium: avocado, salmon, spinach, mushrooms
🥬Magnesium: pumpkin seeds, almonds, spinach, dark chocolate
✅ First 2 Weeks — Daily Checklist
Hydration & Electrolytes
Drink 2-3 liters of water
Add salt to food (don't be shy)
Drink bone broth (1 cup/day)
Take magnesium before bed
Eat potassium-rich foods
Nutrition
Stay under 20g net carbs
Hit your protein target
Eat enough fat (don't fear it)
Track food for first 2 weeks
Lifestyle
Sleep 7-9 hours
Light exercise only (walking)
Avoid intense workouts week 1
Manage stress levels
Pantry Prep
Remove temptation foods
Stock keto snacks
Prep meals for the week
Have electrolyte drinks ready
Not medical advice. If symptoms are severe or persist beyond 2 weeks, consult your doctor. Keto flu is usually just your body adjusting to burning fat instead of sugar.
