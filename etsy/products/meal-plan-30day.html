30-Day Carnivore
Meal Plan

Simple. Repeatable. Results.

This plan is for anyone who's done reading about carnivore and is ready to do it. No complicated recipes. No exotic ingredients. Just meat, salt, and a system you can follow for 30 days without thinking.

How to use it: Eat what's listed. Swap cuts within the same protein if your store doesn't carry something. Eat until full. Don't count calories. Salt everything.

Week 1: Foundation

Days 1-7

Keep it dead simple. Beef, eggs, salt, water. You're adapting. Don't complicate it.

DayBreakfastLunchDinner
Day 1 4 eggs scrambled in butter, salt 1 lb ground beef (80/20), salt 12 oz ribeye, salt
Day 2 3 eggs fried in tallow, salt 2 beef patties (80/20), salt 12 oz chuck steak, salt
Day 3 4 eggs scrambled, salt 1 lb ground beef, salt 12 oz NY strip, salt
Day 4 3 eggs fried in butter, salt 2 beef patties, salt 12 oz ribeye, salt
Day 5 4 eggs scrambled in tallow, salt 1 lb ground beef, salt 1 lb chuck roast (leftover from slow cook), salt
Day 6 3 eggs fried, salt Leftover chuck roast, salt 12 oz ribeye, salt
Day 7 4 eggs scrambled in butter, salt 2 beef patties, salt 12 oz NY strip, salt
Weekly Prep Tips
  • Sunday batch cook: Brown 3 lbs ground beef and slow-cook a 3 lb chuck roast. That's lunches handled.
  • Eggs: Hard boil a dozen for quick grab-and-go breakfasts if mornings are tight.
  • Salt: 2-3 tsp per day minimum. Salt your food heavily. Sip salted water if you get headaches.
  • Fat matters: Always 80/20 ground beef. Lean meat will make you feel terrible this week.
  • Eat when hungry. Don't force a schedule. Don't skip meals to "speed things up." Eat.

Week 2: Expansion

Days 8-14

Adaptation is easing up. Time to introduce variety. Add one new protein every couple of days so you know what agrees with you.

DayBreakfastLunchDinner
Day 8 4 eggs + 3 strips bacon, salt 1 lb ground beef, salt 12 oz ribeye, salt
Day 9 3 eggs fried in bacon grease, salt 2 pork chops, salt 12 oz NY strip, salt
Day 10 4 eggs scrambled in butter, salt 1 can sardines + 2 beef patties, salt 1 lb lamb shoulder chops, salt
Day 11 3 eggs + 3 strips bacon, salt Leftover lamb, salt 12 oz ribeye, salt
Day 12 4 eggs fried in tallow, salt 8 oz salmon fillet, salt 1 lb ground beef, salt
Day 13 3 eggs + 2 oz beef liver, salt 2 pork chops, salt 12 oz chuck steak, salt
Day 14 4 eggs scrambled in butter, salt 2 beef patties + 1 can sardines, salt 12 oz ribeye, salt
Weekly Prep Tips
  • Pork and lamb: Buy family packs on sale and freeze in meal portions. Much cheaper per pound.
  • Liver intro: 2 oz is plenty to start. Freeze liver, grate it frozen into your eggs. You won't taste it.
  • Sardines: Canned in water or olive oil. Cheapest omega-3 source on the planet. Keep 6 cans stocked.
  • Bacon fat: Save it in a jar. Cook your eggs in it. Free cooking fat, better flavor than butter.
  • If something bothers you, drop it and go back to beef + eggs for 2 days. Then try again or move on.

Week 3: Optimization

Days 15-21

You know what you like and what sits well. Now dial in portions, add bone broth, and experiment with cooking methods.

Weekly Prep Tips
  • Bone broth: Make a big pot from beef marrow bones. Simmer 12-24 hours. Freeze in jars. Morning broth replaces coffee for a lot of people.
  • Reverse sear: Oven at 250F until internal hits 120F, then hard sear in cast iron. Best steak method. Period.
  • Slow cooker Sunday: Throw a 4 lb pork shoulder in with salt. 8 hours on low. Lunches for the week.
  • Liver schedule: 2-4 oz twice this week. That's enough for nutrient density without forcing it.
  • Notice your hunger. By now you might naturally skip breakfast some days. That's fine. Don't force three meals if two feels right.

Week 4: Your Rhythm

Days 22-30

Nine days left. You know what works. This week is about finding the pattern you can repeat forever. Personalize it.

Day 30 4 eggs + bacon, salt 1 lb ground beef, salt 16 oz ribeye. You earned it. Salt.
Weekly Prep Tips
  • Short ribs: Slow cook 3-4 lbs. Season with salt only. Best leftovers you'll ever have.
  • Build your rotation. By now you have 4-5 meals you love. Write them down. That's your permanent meal plan.
  • Stock your freezer. Buy meat on sale, freeze in 1 lb portions. Always have a backup when the fridge is empty.
  • Day 30 audit: How's your energy? Sleep? Mood? Body composition? Write it down. This is your baseline.
  • What's next: Keep going. The protocol doesn't end at 30 days. It starts there.

The Whole Protocol in One Line

Eat meat. Salt it. Drink water. Repeat for 30 days. Measure what matters. Adjust based on data, not opinions.
