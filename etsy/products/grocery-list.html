The Carnivore Grocery List

Your fridge-friendly, take-to-the-store cheat sheet for eating clean animal foods.
carnivoreweekly.com
Tier 1: The Essentials Must-haves every single week
  • Beef
    Ground beef (80/20), ribeye, chuck roast, NY strip
  • Eggs
    Pasture-raised if budget allows. 2-4/day is normal.
  • Salt
    Sea salt or Redmond Real Salt. You need more than you think.
  • Water
    Still or sparkling. Add salt if you're cramping.
  • Butter
    Grass-fed (Kerrygold works). Use it on everything.
Tier 2: Rotation Picks Variety meats to rotate weekly
  • Lamb
    Chops, ground lamb, leg of lamb. Great fatty acid profile.
  • Pork
    Bacon (sugar-free), pork belly, chops, ribs.
  • Fish & Seafood
    Salmon, sardines, shrimp. Wild-caught when possible.
  • Organ Meats
    Liver (beef or chicken), heart. Even once a week counts.
  • Poultry
    Chicken thighs, turkey. Add butter (they're lean).
Tier 3: Smart Add-Ons Optional extras if tolerated
  • Bone Broth
    Homemade or store-bought. Great for electrolytes.
  • Tallow / Ghee
    For cooking. High smoke point, zero seed oils.
  • Cheese
    Hard aged cheeses (parmesan, cheddar). Watch portions.
  • Heavy Cream
    In coffee or cooking. High calorie, so measure if losing weight.
  • Coffee / Tea
    Black is best. Some people cut it during adaptation.

Sarah's Quick Shopping Tips
Buy the fat. 80/20 ground beef, not 90/10. Fatty cuts keep you full and cost less per calorie. Your body runs on fat now.
Costco is your friend. Buy ground beef, eggs, and butter in bulk. Freeze what you don't use this week. A chest freezer pays for itself in two months.
Don't skip salt. Your kidneys dump sodium on carnivore. If you're getting headaches, cramps, or brain fog, it's almost always salt. 2-3 tsp per day is a good starting point.
Organ meats aren't optional. Even 2-3 oz of liver per week covers your vitamin A, B12, copper, and folate. Blend it into ground beef if the texture bothers you.
