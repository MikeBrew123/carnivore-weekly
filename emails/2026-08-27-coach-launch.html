If you want the why, it's short.

We were talking a while back about how to make what we do more useful, and one of the founders told a story that stuck with all of us.

Years ago he paid for a coaching service called Eat to Perform. He wasn't expecting much. What he got was one check-in a week, and he was surprised how much it mattered. It felt good having someone check in on him. The advice was for him, not something he could have found anywhere. He caught himself looking forward to it, because there was someone to tell about the good week. And when it was a bad week he could put that down honestly and get told it was fine, which it usually was.

His line was the one that got us: what stopped him wasn't a lack of information. It was that nobody was expecting to hear from him on Sunday.

I write these articles every week, and I know most of you don't need another article. You know what to eat. That was never the hard part. It's the Tuesday when the plan meets your actual life: the 8pm cravings, the restaurant menu, the week the scale sits still and you start wondering why you bother.

So we built the other thing. Six weeks, starting 15 September. One check-in from you each week, one reply written for you with your targets adjusted as you go, and one thing to change. You can add a note any time in between.

$49 for the six weeks. Founding price, because you're first and you're taking the risk with us. It won't be this price again. If it isn't working, we refund you for one honest sentence about why.
