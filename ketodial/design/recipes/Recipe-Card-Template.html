Garlic-Butter Ribeye
& Charred Asparagus

A 20-minute steakhouse dinner that lands almost exactly on your keto macros — seared ribeye basted in garlic-thyme butter, with blistered asparagus.

AIngredients
  • 2 × 8ozribeye steaks, room temp, patted dry
  • 1 tspflaky sea salt + black pepper
  • 1 tbspavocado oil (high smoke point)
  • 3 tbspgrass-fed butter
  • 4garlic cloves, smashed
  • 3 sprigsfresh thyme
  • 1 bunchasparagus, woody ends trimmed
  • 1 tbspolive oil, to coat
  • ½lemon, for finishing
BMethod
  1. Season & rest. Salt the ribeyes generously and let them sit at room temperature while the pan heats — cold steak steams instead of searing.
  2. Screaming-hot pan. Heat avocado oil in a cast-iron skillet until just smoking. Lay the steaks away from you; don't move them for 3 minutes.
  3. Flip & baste. Flip, then add butter, garlic and thyme. Tilt the pan and spoon the foaming butter over the steaks for 2–3 minutes to your doneness.
  4. Rest the meat. Transfer steaks to a board and rest 5 minutes. Keep the garlic butter in the pan.
  5. Char the asparagus. Toss asparagus in olive oil, then sear in the same pan 4–5 minutes until blistered and tender-crisp.
  6. Finish. Slice the ribeye against the grain, spoon over the pan butter, and squeeze lemon over the asparagus.
Dial-in tip
Hitting your protein but short on fat for the day? Add an extra tablespoon of butter to the baste — it pushes each serving to roughly 53g fat without touching your net carbs. Swap asparagus for green beans 1:1 with no macro change.
