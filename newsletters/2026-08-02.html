Alpha-Gal Syndrome: When a Tick Bite Ends Carnivore

Imagine doing everything right on carnivore and then suddenly red meat starts making you sick. Not a little off, genuinely ill. That's exactly what's happening to people with alpha-gal syndrome, and the scary part is the tick bite that causes it can happen months before you notice a single symptom. Sarah breaks down how this immune response works, who's most at risk, and the specific signs that separate alpha-gal from a bad batch of beef.
