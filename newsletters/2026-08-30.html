What the Community Is Asking This Week
“I've been strict carnivore for almost two years and now I'm getting muscle twitches. Is that normal or is something off?”
Many people find that muscle twitching after extended carnivore eating points straight to electrolytes, magnesium and potassium especially. Sarah's article this week on heart palpitations covers a lot of the same ground, so it's worth a read. If the twitching is persistent or getting worse, a quick check-in with your doctor to look at your labs is always a smart move.
“How much beef liver should I actually be eating each week? Can I just do liver, or do I have to mix it with heart?”
Community members generally report that a few ounces of liver two or three times a week hits the sweet spot without overdoing vitamin A. You don't have to eat heart, but many people find mixing organs adds variety and keeps them from getting bored with the same texture. If you're just starting out with liver, smaller amounts more often tends to be easier on the stomach than a big portion all at once.
“I competed in my first ever fitness competition eating fully carnivore. Is that actually possible at a high level?”
It's not only possible, community members are doing it and reporting they felt their best going into competition day. The carnivore approach gives a lot of people very consistent energy without the carb-loading guesswork that comes with other prep strategies. If you've been wondering whether this way of eating can hold up under real athletic pressure, that's a pretty encouraging answer from someone who just tested it.
Try This Week

Add a pinch of salt to your water, every glass, for the next seven days. If you've had any heart flutters, muscle cramps, or afternoon headaches this week, low sodium is almost always the first place to look on carnivore. It's a small change that many people say makes a noticeable difference within two or three days.
