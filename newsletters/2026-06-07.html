Community Pulse

The hair loss conversation really took off this week, and what struck me was how openly people shared their experiences. Some reported noticeable shedding around months two and three, then full regrowth by month five or six. Others are still in the thick of it and understandably anxious. What I keep coming back to is the biology. Telogen effluvium, which is the medical term for stress-related shedding, happens with any major dietary shift. Your body redirects resources during adaptation. It's not permanent for most people, but I understand why it scares folks. If you're dealing with this right now, you're not alone and it's worth reading the full breakdown we published this week.
